# Account and Data Deletion for QuickBooks Deposit Exception Checker

## Disconnect a QuickBooks company

An organization owner can open **Companies**, choose the connected company, and select **Disconnect**. After confirmation, the Service asks Intuit to revoke the authorization and deletes the stored encrypted access and refresh tokens. Future scans stop until the company is reconnected.

Disconnecting does not delete completed control runs, findings, audit records, exported files, Intuit's own records, or the original QuickBooks accounting records.

## Request account and Service-data deletion

Email [info@worksbienstudios.com](mailto:info@worksbienstudios.com) from the verified account email with the subject **Delete Deposit Checker account**. Include the organization name and write: **DELETE MY DEPOSIT CHECKER ORGANIZATION**.

We will verify identity and organization-owner authority before deleting data. After verification, active connections are revoked and customer-controlled account, workspace, completed-run, finding, and audit information is scheduled for deletion within 30 days. Limited records may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, disputes, tax, accounting, or legal compliance. Backups expire under the applicable backup cycle.

## Copies outside the Service

You must delete CSV, JSON, or PDF exports from devices, cloud drives, email, messages, advisers, and other recipients. WorksBien Studios Inc. cannot recall copies you exported or delete information maintained by Intuit in QuickBooks.

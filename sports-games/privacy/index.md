# Privacy Policy for WorksBien Sports Games

**Effective:** 25 November 2026  
**Applies to:** the HUAWEI Quick Games **Penalty Cup**, **Hoop Shot**, **Racket Rally**, **Batting Star** and **Halfpipe Hero**, version 1.0 (each a "Game", together the "Games"), distributed through Huawei AppGallery.

WorksBien Studios Inc. ("WorksBien Studios," "we," "us," or "our") publishes the Games: short, one-thumb sports games (football penalties, basketball free throws, racket sports, batting and skateboarding). This policy explains what the Games process, what stays on the device, and what Huawei and other providers process separately.

## 1. Plain-language summary

- The Games have no WorksBien account, chat, behavioural analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK, and WorksBien Studios does not run a server that receives gameplay data.
- To play, you sign in with your **HUAWEI ID** through Huawei's game service. Huawei handles the sign-in; the Games do not receive or store your password, and WorksBien Studios does not receive your name, e-mail address or phone number.
- Progress, coins, settings and purchase receipts are stored **only on your device**.
- The Games show **non-personalised** ads through **Huawei Petal Ads** (an optional reward video, a full-screen ad between rounds and a small banner on the menu).
- Optional in-game purchases (for example "Remove Ads" or coin packs) are processed by **HUAWEI In-App Purchases**. WorksBien Studios never sees your card or bank details.
- WorksBien Studios does not sell personal information and does not use the Games for cross-app tracking.

## 2. HUAWEI ID sign-in

When a Game starts, it asks you to sign in with your HUAWEI ID (HUAWEI Account Kit / Game Service). Huawei authenticates you and returns a sign-in result to the Game on your device. The Game uses that result only to unlock play on that device and keeps only a "signed in" flag in local storage. WorksBien Studios does not copy your HUAWEI ID profile, player identifier or account details to any server. Huawei processes your account information under the [Huawei Privacy Statement](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/). You can cancel sign-in at any time; the Game then stays on its home screen and offers sign-in again or an exit.

## 3. Information stored on the device

Each Game stores, in the private web storage of the HUAWEI Quick App engine on your device: your game progress and best scores, coins and boosts, unlocked items, sound and settings choices, whether you have accepted these terms, and a list of completed purchase receipts (product IDs and purchase tokens) so that each purchase is delivered exactly once and "Remove Ads" stays active. This information is not uploaded to WorksBien Studios. It remains until you clear the Quick Game's data, uninstall or remove the Quick Game, or erase the device.

## 4. How the Games are delivered (GitHub Pages)

The Games are HTML5 Quick Games. When you open one, the HUAWEI Quick App engine loads the game files over HTTPS from our hosting on GitHub Pages (lrodeveloperr.github.io). Like any web server, GitHub may log technical request data such as your IP address, browser/engine version and the files requested, for security and operation of its service, under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). WorksBien Studios does not use cookies, analytics or tracking on these pages.

## 5. Advertising through Huawei Petal Ads

The Games use Huawei Petal Ads (provided by the HUAWEI Quick App engine) to show:

- an **optional reward video** that plays only when you tap a "watch ad" button for a bonus;
- an occasional **full-screen ad** at a natural break between rounds; and
- a **small banner** on the menu screen.

Every ad request is marked **non-personalised**. WorksBien Studios does not pass your name, contact details, HUAWEI ID or gameplay to Huawei for advertising. To deliver, frequency-cap, measure and protect ads against fraud, Huawei may process information such as device model and operating-system version, the Game's package name and version, language and approximate region derived from network information, IP address, the Open Anonymous Device Identifier (OAID) where available and not restricted, and ad impressions and clicks, under the [Petal Ads privacy statement](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/) and Huawei's other applicable terms. You can reset or restrict the OAID and limit ad tracking in your device's privacy settings. Buying "Remove Ads" removes the full-screen and banner ads; optional reward videos remain available. Tapping an ad may open an advertiser's page or app, whose own terms and privacy policy apply.

## 6. In-game purchases (HUAWEI IAP)

The Games offer optional purchases such as **Remove Ads**, a **Starter Pack** and **coin packs**. Purchases are processed entirely by HUAWEI In-App Purchases and Huawei's payment partners, which handle your payment method, billing, taxes, refunds and fraud prevention under Huawei's terms and privacy statement. The Game receives only the purchase result (product ID, purchase status and a purchase token), which the HUAWEI IAP service on the device checks against our app's public key before the item is delivered. WorksBien Studios does not receive your card or bank details. Refund requests are handled by Huawei according to its policies; you may also contact us.

## 7. Permissions

The Games need network access to load the game and ads and to process sign-in and purchases. They do not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone or file-storage permissions.

## 8. Sharing, sale, tracking and retention

WorksBien Studios does not receive, sell, rent or share players' Game information and does not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Huawei (AppGallery, Quick App engine, HUAWEI ID, Petal Ads, IAP), GitHub, your device manufacturer and your network provider act as independent providers of their own services. We keep support e-mails only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, legal, tax or accounting purposes.

## 9. Your choices, deleting data, and children

WorksBien Studios cannot access, correct, export or delete Game information it never receives. You can delete all Game data on your device by clearing the Quick Game's data or removing the Quick Game in the device's Quick App centre. To manage or delete your HUAWEI ID data, ad identifiers or purchase records held by Huawei, use Huawei's account and privacy settings or contact Huawei. For anything held by WorksBien Studios (such as a support e-mail), contact **info@worksbienstudios.com**; we may need to verify your identity or authority first.

The Games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested as non-personalised. No device or system is completely secure; please protect your device with appropriate access controls.

## 10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the Games, the law or our data practices change. The current version is published at this address with its effective date, and material changes will receive an appropriate in-game or store notice where required.

Operator and privacy contact: **WorksBien Studios Inc.**, **info@worksbienstudios.com**. Customer service: [worksbienstudios.com/customerservice](https://worksbienstudios.com/customerservice).

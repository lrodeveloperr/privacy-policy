# Subscription Terms for LED Light Controller Remote

**Effective:** 10 September 2026  
**Applies to:** LED Light Controller Remote 1.0 for iPhone and iPad

## Free version

The free version includes one saved light, basic power, colour and brightness controls, and the built-in scenes. It does not expire merely because the user declines the subscription.

## LED Controller Pro

**LED Controller Pro** is an optional monthly auto-renewable subscription. While active, it provides:

- unlimited saved lights;
- control of saved lights in groups; and
- unlimited custom scenes.

The United States base price is **US$4.99 per month**. Prices in other storefronts are shown by Apple in the local currency before confirmation and may include applicable taxes. There is no free trial or introductory offer unless Apple explicitly displays one in the purchase sheet.

<p class="notice"><strong>Check compatibility first:</strong> Subscription access does not make an unsupported controller compatible. Use the free compatibility result and physical light response before subscribing. Demo Mode does not prove hardware compatibility.</p>

## Billing and renewal

Apple charges the user's Apple Account after purchase confirmation. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each monthly billing period until cancelled. Apple presents the final price, billing period and any storefront-specific terms before the user authorizes payment.

## Cancellation, expiration and refunds

The user can manage or cancel the subscription in App Store subscription settings. Deleting the App does not cancel it. Cancellation stops future renewals; access ordinarily continues until the end of the current paid period. Pro features become unavailable when the verified entitlement expires or is revoked, subject to any billing-retry or grace-period state provided by Apple.

Apple handles payment authorization, billing, taxes, refunds and purchase-history records. Refund eligibility is determined under Apple's processes and applicable law. These terms do not limit mandatory consumer rights.

Expiration or revocation does not automatically delete saved lights, groups or custom scenes. The user can choose one saved light for free control. Other Pro-created records remain visible but unavailable until an active entitlement is restored or the user deletes them.

## Restore subscription

Use **Settings → Restore subscription** to ask the App Store to restore an eligible active subscription for the same Apple Account. Subscription restoration is separate from iCloud or Finder device-backup restoration.

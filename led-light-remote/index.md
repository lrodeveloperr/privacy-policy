# LED Light Controller Remote Legal and Support

These documents apply to **LED Light Controller Remote 1.0 for iPhone and iPad**, published by WorksBien Studios Inc.

- [Privacy Policy]({{ '/led-light-remote/privacy/' | relative_url }})
- [Supplemental Terms of Use]({{ '/led-light-remote/terms/' | relative_url }})
- [Subscription Terms]({{ '/led-light-remote/purchases/' | relative_url }})
- [Support and Local Data Removal]({{ '/led-light-remote/support/' | relative_url }})

The App does not require a WorksBien account and contains no advertising, behavioural analytics or cross-app tracking. It communicates directly over Bluetooth with a controller chosen by the user. Saved lights, groups, scenes and preferences remain in the App's local storage and may be included in an encrypted iCloud or Finder device backup controlled by the user and Apple. WorksBien Studios does not operate a separate backup or synchronization service.

The free version supports one saved light, basic light controls and built-in scenes. **LED Controller Pro** is an optional auto-renewable subscription. The United States base price is **US$4.99 per month**; the App Store shows the actual localized price before confirmation. Pro provides unlimited saved lights, groups and custom scenes while the subscription is active. There is no free trial.

<p class="notice"><strong>Compatibility:</strong> Bluetooth controller hardware and firmware can vary, including within products sold under the same name. Check the compatibility result and physical response on the actual light before subscribing. Demo Mode is simulated and does not prove compatibility.</p>

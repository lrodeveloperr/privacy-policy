## Product and purchase boundary

The app has no developer-operated account, advertising, analytics, tracking or cloud service. Commands travel directly between the Apple device and the selected television. The app offers an opt-in free **1-day Trial** that lasts 24 hours, does not renew and causes no automatic charge. A separate **Full Remote Unlock** is a one-time non-consumable purchase. There is no subscription.

<p class="notice"><strong>Compatibility:</strong> A compatible Vizio SmartCast television, a private IPv4 address and a working local network are required for real-TV control. The app is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Vizio, Inc.</p>

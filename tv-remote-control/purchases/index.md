# Trial and Purchase Terms for Remote for Vizio TV Controller

**Effective:** 8 September 2026  
**Applies to:** Remote for Vizio TV Controller 1.0 for iPhone and iPad

## 1. Free 1-day Trial

The **1-day Trial** is a free, opt-in, non-consumable App Store product. It begins when Apple completes and StoreKit verifies the transaction. It unlocks all remote features for 24 hours from the verified purchase date, then access ends.

The trial does **not** renew, does **not** convert automatically to the paid unlock and causes **no automatic charge**. Starting or ending the trial does not buy the Full Remote Unlock. A trial previously acquired with the Apple Account may not be available to acquire again.

## 2. One-time Full Remote Unlock

The **Full Remote Unlock** is a separate, paid, non-consumable in-app purchase. It permanently unlocks remote features in supported versions when StoreKit verifies an eligible entitlement. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and has no automatic renewal or recurring Remote for Vizio TV Controller charge.

The App Store purchase sheet displays the localized price, currency and applicable tax before confirmation. The price and terms Apple displays at checkout control. A US $2.99 reference price may differ by storefront, tax, exchange rate or later price change.

## 3. Verification, restoration and revocation

The App grants access only from verified StoreKit information. A pending, cancelled, unverified, refunded or revoked transaction does not grant or retain access. Use **Restore Purchases** with the Apple Account that made an eligible purchase. Apple's account, storefront, redownload and Family Sharing rules determine restoration availability.

Restoring a purchase restores only the StoreKit entitlement. It does not pair a television or restore saved television data. If an entitlement is refunded or revoked, the App may remove paid access.

## 4. Payment, refunds and availability

Apple processes payment, receipts, tax, currency, regional availability and refund decisions. WorksBien Studios does not receive full card or bank details and cannot directly issue an App Store refund. Request an eligible refund through [Apple's Report a Problem service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) or Apple Support. Nothing here limits a mandatory statutory remedy.

The unlock does not include a WorksBien cloud account, hosted remote service, television, network equipment, manufacturer support or a promise of perpetual compatibility with future televisions, firmware, Apple devices or operating systems.

## 5. TestFlight and purchase support

Authorized TestFlight builds use Apple's sandbox purchase environment. Sandbox transactions do not prove a production purchase, may be reset or expire, and do not create a production charge.

For an App entitlement or restore defect, email **info@worksbienstudios.com** with the App version/build, device and operating-system version, storefront, neutral error text and a redacted transaction reference only if necessary. Never send Apple Account credentials, payment-card details, pairing tokens or private network information.

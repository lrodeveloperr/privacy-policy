# Privacy Policy for Rock Paper Scissors Challenge

Effective November 16, 2026

WorksBien Studios Inc. publishes Rock Paper Scissors Challenge for Android. This policy explains how the game handles information during play, advertising and support.

## On your device

The game has no account, sign-in or in-app purchases. It keeps your sound preference and recent computer-match results on your device. The results help adjust the computer opponent. WorksBien Studios does not receive your game moves, scores or settings. You can clear app storage or uninstall the game to remove its local data. Android may retain a separate backup according to your device settings.

## Playing with someone nearby

When you choose Bluetooth or hotspot play, the game uses local communications to find the other phone and run your match. On your local connection, the phones exchange device model names, temporary discovery and connection information, and game messages such as moves and rematch requests. Hotspot and Wi-Fi discovery uses your local network. WorksBien Studios does not receive these messages or operate a match server.

The game requests nearby-device and Bluetooth permissions when you choose Bluetooth play. Some Android versions also require location permission to scan for nearby devices. The game does not use that permission to obtain your physical location.

Google Play services Nearby Connections may collect discovery and connection performance measurements, device model, country, Android build version and the game’s package name to improve the connection service. You can control this collection in your device’s Google Usage & diagnostics settings. [Google’s Nearby Connections data collection description](https://developers.google.com/nearby/connections/overview#data_collection).

## Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob for banner and interstitial ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, app and ad interactions, device or account identifiers, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google encrypts the data it receives from the SDK in transit. [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where required, the game requests your advertising choices through Google's consent form. You can reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings. [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Support, retention and deletion

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We keep support correspondence while it is needed to resolve the issue and meet our legal obligations. For a request to access or delete information we hold, email the address below. Local game data is managed on your device. Google handles information it collects under its own privacy policy and retention practices.

## Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older.

# WiFi Endoscope Camera Viewer

**WiFi Endoscope Camera Viewer** (shown as **ScopeLens** inside the app) is a local-first iPhone utility from **WorksBien Studios Inc.** for viewing and recording compatible Wi-Fi inspection-camera feeds.

## What it supports

- common raw TCP MJPEG cameras, including devices that use `192.168.10.123:7060`;
- HTTP multipart MJPEG streams;
- automatic detection and manual host, port, path and transport setup;
- live view, rotate and mirror controls; and
- local JPEG photos and H.264 video recordings.

Camera protocols vary by model. The app does not support RTSP-only cameras or proprietary encrypted, authenticated or cloud-only protocols. Use the free compatibility preview to verify a camera before purchasing.

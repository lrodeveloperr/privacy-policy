# Privacy Policy

**WiFi Endoscope Camera Viewer** (shown as **ScopeLens** inside the app) is provided by **WorksBien Studios Inc.** This policy is effective September 8, 2026.

## Privacy summary

The app does not require an account and does not collect, sell or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking SDK or developer-operated cloud service.

## Local camera connection

The app connects directly to a compatible inspection camera on a private local Wi-Fi network. Camera frames are processed on the iPhone. They are not sent to WorksBien Studios Inc. or to a developer server.

The app supports specified raw TCP MJPEG and HTTP MJPEG streams. It does not use the camera feed for identification, profiling or advertising.

## Data stored on the device

The app may store the following in its app container:

- the local camera name, private IP address, port, stream path and transport selected by the user;
- compatibility-preview usage state;
- photos and video recordings created by the user; and
- purchase entitlement status supplied by Apple.

This information remains on the device unless the user deliberately shares a capture, transfers a file through iOS, or includes app data in a device backup controlled by the user and Apple.

## Purchases

Apple processes the Lifetime Unlock. WorksBien Studios Inc. receives entitlement status but does not receive payment-card details. Apple's privacy practices and App Store terms apply to Apple Account and transaction data.

## Sharing and disclosure

The app does not disclose camera feeds, captures or saved connection details to WorksBien Studios Inc. A user can choose to export a capture through the standard iOS share sheet; the destination selected by the user then applies its own terms and privacy practices.

## Retention and deletion

Delete individual captures from the Captures screen. Deleting the app removes its local app container, subject to iOS backup and restoration behavior. Apple controls App Store transaction history and restored entitlements.

## Permissions

Local Network access is used only to discover and connect to a compatible camera. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone or advertising-tracking permission.

## Children and sensitive use

The app is a general inspection utility and is not designed to collect information from children. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or life-safety decisions.

# Purchases and Refunds

## Free compatibility preview

The app includes one accumulated compatibility preview of up to 90 seconds of valid live video. The timer starts only after the first valid frame arrives.

The preview is free. It is not an Apple trial or an in-app purchase, does not renew and never charges automatically.

## Lifetime Unlock

**Lifetime Unlock** is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. It permanently unlocks:

- unlimited live viewing; and
- local photo and video capture.

It is not a subscription. The App Store purchase sheet displays Apple's current localized price before confirmation. The United States base price is **US$7.99**; Apple may provide comparable prices in other storefronts.

## Buying while connected to a camera

A camera-created Wi-Fi network may not provide internet access. First verify the camera during the free compatibility preview. The app remembers a successful test and endpoint. Then:

1. switch temporarily to cellular data or an internet-connected Wi-Fi network;
2. complete the purchase or tap Restore Purchase; and
3. reconnect to the camera's Wi-Fi network.

## Restore Purchase

Use Restore Purchase in the app while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple supplies the entitlement; WorksBien Studios Inc. does not receive payment-card details.

## Billing and refunds

Apple processes all payments, receipts, regional taxes, purchase history and refunds. WorksBien Studios Inc. cannot issue App Store refunds directly. To request a refund or report a billing problem, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Compatibility

Purchasing does not make an unsupported camera compatible. Verify live video during the free preview before buying. Supported formats are common raw TCP MJPEG and HTTP multipart MJPEG; RTSP-only and proprietary encrypted, authenticated or cloud-only cameras are not supported.

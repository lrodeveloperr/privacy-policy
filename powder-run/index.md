# Powder Run — Legal and Support

**Operator:** WorksBien Studios Inc., Ontario, Canada  
**Effective date:** 24 September 2026  
**Contact:** [info@worksbienstudios.com](mailto:info@worksbienstudios.com)

Powder Run is an iPhone and iPad shop production recordkeeping app for powder-coating runs in batch ovens. It stores records locally, includes five free first-pass runs, and offers monthly or yearly auto-renewable Pro subscriptions for creating new jobs after the free limit. The app does not measure temperature or certify cure.

- [Support and Data Deletion](support/)

An App Store purchase is handled by Apple at the price shown for your storefront. Existing jobs, rework and exports remain available if the subscription lapses. For app help, use the support page or the contact address above.

# Privacy Policy for Working Life: City Job Sim

**Effective:** 25 November 2026  
**Applies to:** Working Life: City Job Sim 1.0 for Android (Google Play and Huawei AppGallery)

WorksBien Studios Inc. ("WorksBien Studios," "we," "us," or "our") publishes Working Life: City Job Sim (the "Game"), a life simulation where you work shifts, pay the rent and build a better life on a city street. This policy explains what the Game processes, what stays on the device, and what the advertising provider processes separately to show ads.

## 1. Plain-language summary

- The Game has no WorksBien account, sign-in, chat, in-app purchases, behavioural analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK.
- Game progress and settings are stored only on the player's device. The Game does not send gameplay, progress, or settings to a WorksBien server.
- The Game shows advertising: a full-screen ad may appear after a completed level, and players can choose to watch a video ad for an in-game bonus. We request **non-personalised** ads only.
- On Google Play the ads come from Google AdMob; on Huawei AppGallery they come from Huawei Petal Ads. The advertising provider processes limited device and network information to deliver, measure, and protect ads under its own privacy policy.
- WorksBien Studios does not sell personal information and does not use the Game for cross-app tracking.

## 2. Information stored on the device

The Game stores the player's progress (such as cash, debt, skill, energy, housing and day), the current daily and weekly goal status, the chosen language, graphics quality, sound, music, and vibration settings, and which first-time tips have been shown. This information is kept in the Game's private application storage on the device.

It stays on the device and is not uploaded to WorksBien Studios. It remains until the player clears the Game's data, uninstalls the Game, or erases the device, subject to any device backup the player has enabled through the operating system.

## 3. Advertising

We configure every ad request as **non-personalised** and limit ad content to a rating suitable for teens. WorksBien Studios does not pass the player's name, contact details, or gameplay to the advertising provider.

**Google Play version — Google AdMob.** To deliver, frequency-cap, measure, and protect ads against fraud, Google may process information such as the device model and operating-system version, the app's package name and version, language and approximate location derived from the IP address, the IP address itself, the device's advertising ID where the player has not deleted or restricted it, ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostic information about ad performance. Google processes this information under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and explains its use in apps at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Players can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings.

**Huawei AppGallery version — Huawei Petal Ads.** Huawei may process information such as the device model and operating-system version, the app's package name and version, language and approximate region derived from network information, IP address, the Open Anonymous Device Identifier (OAID) where the device provides one and the player has not restricted it, and ad impressions and clicks, under the [Petal Ads privacy statement](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/) and Huawei's other applicable terms. Players can reset or restrict the OAID and limit ad tracking in their device's privacy settings.

Tapping an advertisement may open a web page, an app store page, or another app operated by the advertiser, whose own terms and privacy policy apply.

## 4. Network access and permissions

The Game requests internet access only so advertisements can load. Gameplay works without a connection; when offline, no ads are shown and optional video bonuses are unavailable. The Game may use vibration for optional haptic feedback, which can be switched on or off in the Game's settings. On Google Play, the advertising SDK declares the advertising ID permission. The Game does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions.

## 5. Sharing, sale, tracking, and retention

WorksBien Studios does not receive, sell, rent, or share players' Game information, and does not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Google, Huawei, the app stores, the device manufacturer, the operating-system vendor, and network providers are independent providers for their own services. We may retain support correspondence only as reasonably needed for support, security, legal, tax, or accounting purposes.

## 6. Privacy requests, children, and security

WorksBien Studios cannot access, correct, export, or delete Game information that it never receives. Players can delete their Game data by clearing the Game's storage in the device settings or by uninstalling the Game. For information an advertising provider holds, use that provider's privacy controls and contact options.

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested as non-personalised and limited to a teen-appropriate content rating. No device or system is completely secure; players should protect their devices with appropriate access controls.

For information actually held by WorksBien Studios, such as a support email, contact **info@worksbienstudios.com**. We may need to verify identity or authority before acting on a request.

## 7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the Game, law, or data practices change. The current version is published at this URL with its effective date. Material changes will receive an appropriate Game or store notice where required.

Operator and privacy contact: **WorksBien Studios Inc.**, **info@worksbienstudios.com**. Customer service: [worksbienstudios.com/customerservice](https://worksbienstudios.com/customerservice).

## Product and purchase boundary

The App stores user-entered inventory items, part numbers, categories, locations, quantities, restock levels and activity history in app-private storage on the device. It has no GoodUse Studios account, publisher inventory cloud, advertising, behavioural analytics or third-party tracking SDK.

Up to **10 active inventory items** may be kept without payment. Adding an eleventh active item requires a **one-time, non-consumable lifetime unlock** sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. It is not a subscription and does not renew. Quantity changes, stock uses, restocks and activity entries are not the cap unit. Existing records remain available if the unlock is absent or later unavailable.

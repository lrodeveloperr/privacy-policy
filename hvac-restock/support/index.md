# Support and Data Deletion for HVAC Restock

**Effective:** 6 September 2026  
**Applies to:** HVAC Restock 1.0 for iPhone and iPad

## 1. Support contact

Email: **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**  
Postal address: 36 Zorra Street, Toronto (Etobicoke), Ontario M8Z 0G5, Canada

Include the App version/build, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, storefront, neutral error text and non-sensitive reproduction steps. Do **not** send screenshots containing inventory, CSV files, customer or employee information, credentials, payment-card details, or confidential operational records.

HVAC Restock has no customer account or GoodUse Studios inventory cloud. We cannot view, retrieve, edit, restore or remotely delete records held only on a device.

## 2. Export before deletion

If inventory rows are needed, use **Settings → Export inventory CSV** and save the file to an authorized location before deleting data. The CSV is not a complete backup: it does not preserve the activity history, reversals, onboarding state or App Store entitlement. Verify the exported file before relying on it and protect it at its destination.

## 3. Delete local App data

Open **Settings → Delete all app data**, review the warning and confirm. This deletes inventory items and activity held by the App on that device. A lifetime purchase remains associated with the eligible Apple Account and can be restored through **Settings → Restore purchase**.

Uninstalling the App removes App-contained data according to Apple platform behaviour. Neither action deletes CSV files already exported or shared, copies in device or iCloud backups, Apple's transaction records, or support correspondence.

## 4. Delete external copies and backups

Delete exported CSV files from each Files location, cloud provider, message, email, recipient, external device or other destination. Manage Apple device and iCloud backups through Apple settings and account tools. GoodUse Studios cannot recall or remotely wipe those copies.

## 5. Restore purchase

Open **Settings → Restore purchase** while signed into the Apple Account that made the eligible purchase. Restoration requires Apple services and restores the entitlement only, not inventory data. For App Store payment, receipt or refund help, use Apple support or [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## 6. Privacy and security requests

For records held only on a device, use the local deletion controls above. For support, privacy or security correspondence actually held by GoodUse Studios, email **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com** from the same address where practical and state the request. We may verify identity or authority and may retain limited records where required for security, legal, tax, accounting or dispute purposes.

For a security report, send a non-sensitive description and safe reproduction steps. Do not include live business data, credentials, destructive payloads or sensitive personal information.

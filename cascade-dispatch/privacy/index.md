# Cascade Dispatch Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 21, 2026**

Cascade Dispatch is published by **GoodUse Studios**, Ontario, Canada. This policy describes how the mobile game handles information.

## Device-local game data

Cascade Dispatch does not require a game account. Game progress, settings, selected language, attempt-recharge timestamps, local records, and a local cache of verified premium status are stored on your device. GoodUse Studios does not operate a Cascade Dispatch gameplay server and does not receive your puzzle history through the game.

You can delete device-local game data using your device's app-storage controls or by uninstalling the app, subject to the operating system's normal backup and restore behavior.

## Advertising in free mode

The free version can display advertising supplied by **Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads**. The app is configured to request **non-personalized advertising** and Google's restricted-data-processing signal, and it disables the publisher first-party identifier. Where Google's User Messaging Platform requires a privacy or consent choice, the app obtains that state before requesting an ad and provides access to the ad privacy-options form when available.

Even when ads are non-personalized, Google and its advertising technology may process information needed to deliver, secure, measure, and limit ads. Depending on platform, device, region, consent state, and Google's current SDK practices, this can include IP address or coarse region, device and app information, operating-system information, ad delivery/impression/interaction information, diagnostics, and consent or advertising-related identifiers where permitted. GoodUse Studios does not use Cascade Dispatch gameplay history to build advertising profiles.

Google's privacy information is available at <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and information about Google's advertising technology is available at <https://business.safety.google/privacy/>.

## Purchases

Cascade Dispatch offers a lifetime **Unlimited Play** non-consumable purchase. Purchases are processed by Google Play on Android and the App Store on iOS. Those stores handle payment details, store-account information, receipts, refunds, and transaction records under their own policies. Cascade Dispatch does not collect or store payment-card details.

The app asks the applicable store to verify whether the lifetime product is currently owned. A local entitlement cache may be stored so already-verified premium status can continue to work when temporary store verification is unavailable. A later successful store verification can update that cache, including after a refund or entitlement change.

Google Play privacy information: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>  
Apple privacy information: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

## Data sharing

GoodUse Studios does not sell Cascade Dispatch gameplay data. Information can be processed by Google or Apple only as described above for advertising, purchase processing, fraud/security, and related platform functionality. Their processing is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

## Retention and deletion

Device-local game data remains until you clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes it, subject to device backup/restore behavior. Purchase records are retained by the applicable app store under that store's policies. Advertising data processed by Google is retained under Google's applicable policies and settings.

To ask GoodUse Studios a privacy question, contact **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**. Because the game has no GoodUse account or gameplay server, GoodUse Studios ordinarily has no server-side gameplay profile to retrieve or delete.

## Your choices

You can:

- use the in-app **Ad Privacy Choices** control when Google's privacy-options form is available;
- purchase Unlimited Play to remove in-game advertising and the attempt limit;
- clear or delete local game data through your device controls; and
- contact GoodUse Studios at **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com** with a privacy request or question.

## Changes

If Cascade Dispatch's data practices or third-party SDKs materially change, this policy will be updated and the store privacy disclosures will be reviewed for consistency.

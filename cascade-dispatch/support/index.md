# Cascade Dispatch Support

Cascade Dispatch is published by **GoodUse Studios**. For support, contact **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**.

## Gameplay

- Progress is sequential. Clearing the current crisis unlocks the next one.
- Free mode has five attempts. Each used attempt returns independently one hour after it was used.
- A failed reset or timeout discards that grid. A retry generates a fresh deterministic puzzle and does not reveal the prior answer.
- Unlimited Play removes the attempt limit and in-game ads.

## Purchases

If Unlimited Play was already purchased but is not showing, open **Settings → Restore Purchase** while signed into the same Google Play or Apple account that owns the purchase. Store availability and account verification are required for restoration.

## Advertising privacy

Free-mode ads are requested as non-personalized. Open **Settings → Ad Privacy Choices** to view or change Google's privacy choices when the option is available in your region/session.

## Local data

Cascade Dispatch does not require an account. Progress and settings are stored locally. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove local progress, subject to the device operating system's backup/restore behavior.

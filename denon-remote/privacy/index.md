# Privacy Policy for Remote for Denon & Marantz

**Effective date:** 11 September 2026  
**Developer:** WorksBien Studios Inc.

This Privacy Policy explains how Remote for Denon & Marantz handles information. The app is designed to work locally between your iPhone or iPad and a compatible receiver on the same network.

## Privacy summary

WorksBien Studios Inc. does not collect personal data from the app. The app does not contain accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, a developer-operated cloud service, or an external application server. WorksBien Studios Inc. does not sell or rent personal data.

## Local network use

The app requests Local Network permission only when you choose to connect to a real receiver. It uses that access to find or contact compatible receivers and to send control commands on your local network. Direct-IP connections and optional Bonjour discovery remain within your local network. Receiver commands are not routed through WorksBien Studios Inc.

Demo Remote does not request Local Network permission and sends no network traffic.

## Information stored on your device

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

- receiver addresses and room names;
- receiver and connection settings;
- volume-safety preferences;
- selected language and other app preferences;
- trial start, end, and last-seen timestamps; and
- App Store entitlement status made available through StoreKit.

This information is used only to provide the app's features. WorksBien Studios Inc. cannot access it remotely. Depending on your Apple backup settings, locally stored app data may be included in a device backup managed by Apple.

## Diagnostics and support

The app creates a diagnostic report only when you request one. The generated report excludes receiver IP addresses and room names. It remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.

If you contact support or share a diagnostic report, the information you choose to provide is sent through the service or app you select. The app does not send support information automatically. The privacy terms of the selected service also apply.

## Purchases

Annual access and lifetime access are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app receives signed transaction and entitlement information from StoreKit so it can unlock purchased access. WorksBien Studios Inc. does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Apple may provide WorksBien Studios Inc. with transaction, sales, and financial reports under Apple's policies.

## Retention and deletion

Receiver records and their settings remain on your device until you delete the receiver or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local receiver database.

A device-only Keychain timestamp may remain on that device after the app is deleted to prevent repeated seven-day trials. WorksBien Studios Inc. cannot view or retrieve that timestamp. App Store purchase history and entitlements are retained and managed by Apple under Apple's policies.

If you have contacted WorksBien Studios Inc. for support, you may use the contact method below to request access to or deletion of personal information contained in that support correspondence, subject to applicable law.

## Permissions

The app uses Local Network permission for real-receiver control. It does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or advertising identifiers.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose home-entertainment utility and is not directed to children. Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

## Your privacy rights

Privacy rights vary by location. If you have provided personal information directly to WorksBien Studios Inc. through support, you may contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection rights that apply to you.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Changes apply prospectively from the stated effective date.

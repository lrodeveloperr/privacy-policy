# Promise Guard data deletion and no-account explanation

**Publisher:** GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada  
**Effective and last updated:** 21 August 2026

## No GoodUse Studios account

Promise Guard does not require or create a GoodUse Studios account. Promise-record content is stored on the device by default, not in a GoodUse Studios record-content server. There is therefore normally no remote Promise Guard account or server-side record-content profile for GoodUse Studios to delete on request.

## Delete one record

Use the app's delete action for the selected record and confirm the request. The app plans removal of the selected record, its locally managed supporting-file references and related reminders. Deleting a record does not restore a used free-record creation slot.

## Delete all local app data

Use the app's available all-data deletion control where present. Alternatively, uninstall Promise Guard and choose any operating-system option that removes the app's local data. Platform behaviour can vary, so consult the device provider's instructions.

## Delete exported or shared copies

Deleting local app data does not delete copies you exported, shared, emailed, printed or placed in device/cloud backup. Delete those copies from each destination and, where appropriate, its trash or recovery folder. Recipients control copies already sent to them.

## Purchases are separate

Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel an Android subscription, erase store purchase history or revoke an iOS non-consumable purchase. Manage subscriptions and purchase history through Apple or Google under their policies.

## Need help?

Email [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com) and identify Promise Guard and your platform. Support cannot retrieve or delete record content it does not possess.

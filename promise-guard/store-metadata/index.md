## Coverage

- **31 selectable in-app languages**
- **34 App Store Connect localizations**
- **37 Google Play localizations**

The store count is larger than the language count because English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and Chinese have region- or script-specific storefront variants. The product registry has no exact App Store Connect metadata locale for Urdu, Bengali or Filipino; those languages remain localized in the app and have Google Play listings, while the App Store uses its configured primary-language fallback.

## Positioning rules used throughout

- factual, calm and non-accusatory language;
- no claim that an entry proves a promise or breach;
- no legal-advice or guaranteed-outcome claim;
- local-first records, with user-initiated export, backup and sharing;
- no required GoodUse Studios account or record-content server;
- no advertising;
- two complete lifetime-created records available without paid access;
- iOS: one-time non-consumable unlock for additional record creation;
- Android: monthly auto-renewing subscription only for additional record creation;
- existing records remain usable without an active paid creation entitlement; and
- storefront-localized price and purchase terms are authoritative.

# Promise Guard store submission checklist

The listing text is drafted and length-validated. The following fields must be completed or revalidated against the final signed binaries and publisher account before submission.

## Verified publisher details still required

- Confirm the public legal/street address and telephone number, if required for the App Store Support URL, EU Digital Services Act trader disclosure or local consumer law. The published pages currently use the verified contact available in the repository: `lrodeveloperr@gmail.com`, GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada.
- Confirm whether `lrodeveloperr@gmail.com` is the long-term public support and privacy contact.
- Confirm the copyright holder wording and year shown in each console.

## Binary-dependent declarations

- Final bundle ID/package name, SKU, version and build number.
- Final SDK and dependency inventory before completing Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data safety declarations.
- Actual permissions in each signed binary, including notifications and file/document access.
- Whether operating-system cloud backup includes local app data under the final platform configuration.
- Encryption/export-compliance answers based on the final implementation.
- Age rating/questionnaire based on final content and external links.
- Accessibility, narrow-device and RTL checks against final screenshots and production fonts.

## Commercial setup

- iOS non-consumable product ID and localized purchase display metadata; US reference price **$39.99**, with App Store localized price authoritative.
- Android subscription product ID/base plan; monthly only; US reference price **$3.99/month**, with Google Play localized price authoritative.
- No iOS subscription, Android annual plan, Android lifetime purchase, free trial or advertising.
- Test purchase, restore, renewal, grace/expiry, cancellation and offline-entitlement behaviour using store sandbox/test tracks.

## Listing assets

- Final icon from the approved icon set.
- Device-accurate screenshots for required sizes and priority locales.
- Screenshot captions that use the same factual, non-accusatory terminology as the JSON listings.
- Optional App Preview/feature graphic only if it shows the final app and does not imply verification, legal proof or guaranteed recovery.

## Final console review

- Use the public [Privacy Policy](../privacy/), [Support](../support/) and [product/legal overview](../) URLs.
- Preview every localization and check truncation, punctuation, RTL order and line breaks.
- Confirm the store purchase sheet—not listing prose—shows the authoritative localized price and renewal terms.
- Submit listings only after the final binary behaviour matches every factual claim.

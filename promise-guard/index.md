# Promise Guard — legal and support

**Publisher:** GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada  
**Effective and last updated:** 21 August 2026  
**Contact:** [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com)

Promise Guard helps you keep a structured personal record of what a provider promised, what happened next and the supporting items you choose to add.

<p class="notice"><strong>Important:</strong> Promise Guard is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not verify your entries, determine whether a promise was breached, provide legal advice or guarantee that a provider, regulator or court will accept a report.</p>

## Product summary

- Records are stored locally on the device by default.
- No GoodUse Studios account or server is required for record storage.
- The app has no advertising.
- The first two complete lifetime-created records are available without a paid entitlement.
- iOS offers a one-time non-consumable unlock for creating additional records.
- Android offers an auto-renewing monthly subscription for creating additional records.
- Existing records and their record-management features remain usable without an active paid entitlement.
- Store-displayed localized prices and purchase terms are authoritative.

## Contact

For support, identify **Promise Guard**, your platform (iOS or Android), app version and a general description of the problem. Do not email passwords, payment-card information, identity documents or the confidential contents of a promise record.

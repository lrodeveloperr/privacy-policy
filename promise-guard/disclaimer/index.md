# Promise Guard safety and legal disclaimer

**Publisher:** GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada  
**Effective and last updated:** 21 August 2026

Promise Guard is an organizational and personal record-keeping tool. It is not a law firm, claims representative, regulator, dispute-resolution service, emergency service or evidence-verification service.

## Factual personal records only

The app's summaries are generated from information you enter and supporting items you select. The app does not independently verify identity, authenticity, accuracy, completeness, delivery, receipt, legal admissibility or whether a promise was made, changed, fulfilled or breached. A status or comparison shown in the app reflects your recorded facts and deterministic rules, not a legal finding.

## Not professional advice

Nothing in the app or its generated report is legal, financial, insurance, tax, medical or other professional advice. Official routes and external links are provided for convenience and can change. Confirm current requirements with the relevant official body or a qualified professional.

## Review before sharing

Before sending a report or file:

- check dates, amounts, names and quoted wording;
- remove information the recipient does not need;
- confirm that you have the right to share every attachment;
- verify the recipient and delivery method; and
- retain an appropriate copy if needed.

Do not rely on Promise Guard for an emergency, a filing deadline, limitation period, court deadline, safety-critical instruction or a situation requiring immediate professional help. Use the relevant official or emergency channel.

## Respectful and lawful use

Keep entries factual and non-accusatory. Do not use the app to harass, threaten, impersonate, defame, deceive or publish private information unlawfully. Laws and evidentiary rules vary, and Promise Guard does not determine which rules apply.

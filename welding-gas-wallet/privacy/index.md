# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 3 September 2026

**Operator:** GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada

**Contact:** [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com)

## Summary

Welding Gas Wallet is local-first. It does not require a GoodUse Studios account and GoodUse Studios does not operate a server that receives your cylinder records during normal use.

The app can store, on your device, cylinder names, gas type, capacity, serial number, ownership/rental relationship, supplier, acquisition and refill/exchange costs, dates, status, notes, activity history, reminder titles and due dates. These records are not uploaded to a GoodUse Studios server.

The app does **not** scan cylinders or use the camera. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise-location, Bluetooth or broad photo-library access. It does not track consumable lots or certificates.

## No advertising, analytics or tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, tracking or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not use the Android advertising ID or Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, and GoodUse Studios does not build advertising profiles from app activity.

## Purchases

Android Pro is provided through Google Play as auto-renewing monthly and annual subscriptions. iOS Pro is a one-time, non-consumable lifetime purchase through the App Store. Apple and Google process account, billing and payment information under their own policies. GoodUse Studios does not receive full payment-card details. The app receives the product and entitlement status needed to unlock Pro and keeps the resulting entitlement state locally.

## Backups and sharing

You can initiate a wallet backup and choose a file destination. Exported files can contain the cylinder information you entered. The selected file destination, cloud provider, email service or other receiving application is a separate service and its own privacy policy applies. The app does not upload backups to a GoodUse Studios server.

Android system backup or device-transfer services and Apple's device/iCloud backup may separately copy app data according to operating-system settings and capabilities. GoodUse Studios does not control those services.

## Notifications

If you enable device alerts, the app requests notification permission when required and schedules reminders locally. Reminder titles and cylinder names may appear on the lock screen. GoodUse Studios does not receive reminder content and does not operate a push-notification server for the app.

## Deletion

Use **Settings → Delete wallet** to remove the wallet's local records. This does not delete copies you exported elsewhere, third-party records, or store purchase/subscription records. See [Data Deletion](../deletion/).

## Security and rights

We minimize access by keeping core wallet records local, but no storage method is completely secure. Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights concerning information processed by GoodUse Studios. Because wallet content stays local, most control is exercised directly through the app and your device. For a privacy request or complaint concerning information under GoodUse Studios' control, email [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com) and identify the app and platform. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or confidential operational records.

## Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. GoodUse Studios does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

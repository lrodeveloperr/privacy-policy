# Welding Gas Wallet Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada  
**Effective and last updated:** 21 August 2026  
**Contact:** [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com)

This policy explains the data practices of the native Android and iOS Welding
Gas Wallet application. It does not describe the independent practices of
Apple, Google, a device manufacturer, a backup or file destination, or a
supplier whose details you record.

## 1. Information you choose to record

Welding Gas Wallet can store cylinder information you enter, such as a
nickname, gas description, supplier, relationship label, serial or reference,
capacity, costs, event history, notes, reminder details, and lifecycle status.
If a native version offers a photo field, it can also retain a local reference
to a photo you select. You decide what to enter. Do not store information you
do not have the right to possess.

## 2. Local-first storage and no GoodUse Studios account

Cylinder records are stored in the app's local storage on your device. The app
does not require or create a GoodUse Studios account. GoodUse Studios does not
operate a cloud database that receives or stores the contents of your cylinder
wallet as part of normal app use.

Your operating system, device-transfer feature, or backup service may
separately copy app data according to your device settings. Those services are
controlled by their providers, not by GoodUse Studios.

## 3. Collection, advertising, analytics, and tracking

The release described by this policy contains no advertising and is not
designed to track users across apps or websites. It contains no GoodUse Studios
account system and no third-party advertising, analytics, or
developer-operated crash-reporting SDK. GoodUse Studios does not sell cylinder
record content.

Apple and Google independently operate their stores, billing systems, platform
diagnostics, and developer reporting. They may process store-account,
transaction, device, usage, or diagnostic information and may make transaction
or diagnostic reports available to developers under their own terms and
privacy policies. This is separate from GoodUse Studios receiving the contents
of your cylinder wallet.

## 4. Purchases

Apple or Google processes purchases. GoodUse Studios does not receive your full
payment-card details. The app retrieves store-supplied product, price, purchase,
and entitlement status needed to offer, confirm, restore, or manage access. A
limited entitlement cache can be stored on the device. Store purchase history
is governed by the applicable store's policies and cannot be erased by deleting
an app record.

## 5. Notifications and selected files or photos

If you enable reminders, the app asks the operating system to schedule local
notifications. A notification can appear on a lock screen according to your
device settings. Review the title before saving a reminder and adjust lock-screen
visibility if needed.

File or photo access occurs only when you choose an import, export, backup, or
photo action. Where supported, the app uses the system file or photo picker
rather than broad library access. The release does not request location,
contacts, microphone, call-log, or advertising-identifier access.

## 6. Export, import, and sharing

The app does not send a wallet backup unless you initiate an export or sharing
action. A destination you choose can receive and retain the exported file, and
its privacy and retention practices apply. Review a backup before sharing it
and protect it as you would the underlying records.

Import can replace local wallet data after confirmation. Store entitlements are
not imported from a backup and must be verified through Apple or Google. A
backup may exclude locally referenced photos; the app must tell you if they are
not included.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, replace the wallet through a
confirmed import, clear the app's local storage, or delete the app and its data.
The app may keep a local recovery snapshot so that a failed or conflicting
write does not destroy the last safe wallet. That snapshot remains on the
device until it is replaced or the app's local storage is removed.

Deleting local data does not delete files you previously exported, copies held
by a recipient, operating-system backups, or store purchase history. Delete
those copies through each relevant destination. Because GoodUse Studios does
not hold a Welding Gas Wallet account or cylinder-record server, it normally
has no remote cylinder-wallet content to retrieve or delete. See the [local
data deletion explanation](../deletion/).

## 8. Security

Protect the device and any exported backup with an appropriate passcode,
current operating-system software, and secure storage. No storage or transfer
method is completely secure. GoodUse Studios support cannot recover a local
wallet that was deleted without an available user-controlled backup.

## 9. Children

Welding Gas Wallet is an occupational utility designed for adults and is not
directed to children. Do not use it to store a child's personal information.

## 10. International use

The app can be used in multiple languages and regions. The store, operating
system, and any destination you choose can process information in other
countries under their own policies. Mandatory rights in your jurisdiction
remain unaffected.

## 11. Changes

We will update this policy if the app's data practices materially change or
legal requirements require an update. The current version will show its last
updated date. Where required, a material change will be communicated before a
new collection or use begins.

## 12. Contact

Email [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com) with privacy
questions and identify the app and platform. Do not send passwords, full
payment-card information, or unredacted cylinder records unless it is necessary
and you understand the risk.

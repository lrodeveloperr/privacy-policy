# Privacy Policy for Welding Gas Wallet

**Effective:** 22 August 2026  
**Publisher:** GoodUse Studios

This policy explains how Welding Gas Wallet (the “App”) handles information.

## 1. Plain-language summary

- The App does not require a GoodUse Studios account.
- Cylinder records are stored locally on the device. GoodUse Studios does not operate a cylinder-record cloud database for the App.
- The App contains no advertising SDK, behavioural analytics SDK, tracking SDK, or third-party crash-reporting SDK.
- The App does not request location, contacts, microphone, call-log, or broad photo/media access for ordinary operation.
- Information leaves the App when the user deliberately exports or shares a backup, contacts support, opens an external legal/support page, or uses Google Play/App Store purchase services.

## 2. Information stored locally

Depending on the features used, local records can include cylinder nicknames, gas type, capacity, serial number, relationship type, supplier information, acquisition information, refill/exchange/cost history, notes, reminders, app settings, and a pending draft. Users control what they enter.

These records may contain confidential or personal information if a user chooses to enter it. Users should enter only information they are authorized to keep and should avoid unnecessary personal information.

## 3. Why the App processes local information

The App processes local records to display the wallet, preserve cylinder history, calculate user-requested spend summaries, manage reminders, enforce the free editable-cylinder limit, preserve a gated fourth-cylinder draft, and create or import user-requested backups.

## 4. Storage, backup, export, and deletion

The App stores operational records in app-private storage and provides no GoodUse Studios cloud synchronization for those records. The Android build disables Android application backup in its manifest. A user can deliberately export a backup through the operating system’s share/file surfaces. Any exported copy is then controlled by the destination selected by the user, such as a file provider, email app, cloud drive, or another device.

GoodUse Studios cannot retrieve, remotely wipe, or restore cylinder records it never received. The App’s local deletion tools remove App-managed local wallet data on that device, but they do not erase copies already exported or records retained independently by Google Play, Apple, an email provider, a file provider, or another third party.

See [Local Data Deletion]({{ '/welding-gas-wallet/deletion/' | relative_url }}) for the deletion procedure.

## 5. Reminders and notifications

If the user enables reminders, the App requests notification permission when needed and schedules reminder notifications through the device operating system. Reminder content is created from local App records. Notification permission can be changed in device settings.

## 6. Purchases and store information

Google Play or Apple processes store purchases. GoodUse Studios does not receive full payment-card or bank details. The App may receive purchase status, product identifiers, purchase tokens/transaction information, localized prices, and verification material supplied by the store so it can determine access. Store dashboards may separately provide sales, refund, tax, storefront, and aggregate performance information under the store’s own terms.

The App does not use imported backup data as proof of a paid entitlement.

## 7. Support, testing, and policy website information

If a user contacts support, GoodUse Studios receives the email address and information the user chooses to send. Users should provide only the App version/build, device/OS version, locale, a neutral description of the issue, and reproduction steps where possible. Do not send backups, cylinder serial numbers, supplier records, credentials, or payment-card information unless specifically required and safe to share.

Testing platforms such as Google Play or Apple TestFlight may provide tester feedback, device/build information, screenshots, and diagnostics according to the tester’s choices and the platform’s terms.

These policy pages are hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and network providers may process request, security, browser/device, IP-address, and log information under their own policies.

## 8. Sharing

GoodUse Studios does not sell cylinder records and cannot sell or disclose local records it never receives. Information actually received by GoodUse Studios may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to provide requested support, comply with law, protect rights or security, or establish or defend legal claims. Deliberately exported information is processed by the destination selected by the user under that provider’s terms.

## 9. Retention and requests

Local wallet information remains on the device until changed or deleted by the user, the App’s local data is cleared, the App is uninstalled, or the operating system removes the storage. GoodUse Studios retains support correspondence and store/accounting information it actually receives only as reasonably needed for support, security, legal, tax, accounting, or dispute purposes.

For information actually held by GoodUse Studios, contact **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com** to request access, correction, deletion, or another applicable privacy right. GoodUse Studios cannot search or delete local device data it never received.

## 10. Security, international use, and children

The App is a recordkeeping utility and is not directed to children. No storage system is completely secure. Device loss, hardware failure, uninstall, reset, corruption, or loss of an exported backup can make information unavailable. Users are responsible for reasonable device access controls and for protecting exported copies.

## 11. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the App, law, or data practices change. The current version is published at this URL with its effective date.

Privacy and support contact: **GoodUse Studios — lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**.

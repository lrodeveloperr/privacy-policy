# Support for Welding Gas Wallet

**Support contact:** lrodeveloperr@gmail.com

Welding Gas Wallet is designed to keep cylinder records on the device rather than in a GoodUse Studios account. This means GoodUse Studios normally cannot inspect, retrieve, reset, or restore the user’s wallet remotely.

## Before contacting support

For a useful support report, include:

- App version/build;
- Android or iOS version and device model;
- selected App language/locale;
- the exact neutral error message shown by the App;
- steps that reproduce the issue without including operational or personal data.

Do **not** email a wallet backup, cylinder serial numbers, supplier records, credentials, payment-card information, or other confidential information unless it is specifically required and safe to share.

## Common recovery paths

- **Store unavailable:** keep the App installed and retry when the store and network are available. A store outage must not grant paid access.
- **Restore paid access:** use the App’s restore/refresh access control while signed into the store account that owns the purchase.
- **Manage an Android subscription:** use the App’s Manage subscription control or Google Play’s subscription management surface.
- **Notification permission denied:** enable notifications for Welding Gas Wallet in device settings, then enable reminders in the App.
- **Local backup:** use the App’s export function and keep exported copies only in destinations you trust.
- **Import:** use the App’s import function with a valid Welding Gas Wallet backup. Invalid or corrupt imports are rejected rather than partially applied.
- **Corrupt local store:** use the App’s recovery surface to restore from a valid backup or explicitly clear the damaged local wallet.

## Purchases and refunds

Payments, subscription renewals, cancellations, refunds, and store account access are processed by Google Play or Apple as applicable. GoodUse Studios does not receive full card or bank details.

# Welding Gas Wallet Support

**Publisher:** GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada  
**Effective and last updated:** 21 August 2026  
**Support email:** [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com)

## Before contacting support

1. Record whether you use Android or iOS.
2. Record the app and operating-system versions.
3. Restart the app and device.
4. Check whether the selected reminder, photo, file, or notification action has
   the required operating-system permission.
5. For purchase issues, confirm the device is signed in to the store account
   used for the purchase.

## Purchases

- **Android:** Open **Settings → Plan & access → Manage subscription** to view
  or cancel in Google Play. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.
- **iOS:** Use **Restore Purchases** with the Apple account used for the
  one-time unlock.
- **Charges and refunds:** Apple or Google controls store billing and refund
  decisions. Use the store's purchase history and support tools.

## Records, reminders, and backups

Cylinder records are local-first. GoodUse Studios support cannot view or
recover them from a GoodUse Studios server. Before replacing a device,
clearing storage, or deleting the app, create and safely retain a backup where
available. Verify important exports before relying on them. A backup may not
include locally referenced photos and never transfers a store entitlement.

When reporting a defect, provide the steps, expected result, and actual result.
A redacted screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment-card details,
unredacted backups, serial numbers, or confidential operational information
unless specifically necessary and safe.

Support can help with app operation and reproducible defects. It cannot inspect
a cylinder, select a gas, determine ownership or legal status, decide safety or
fillability, interpret test requirements, or guarantee supplier acceptance.

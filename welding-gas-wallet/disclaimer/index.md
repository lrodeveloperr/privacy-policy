# Safety & Scope Disclaimer for Welding Gas Wallet

**Effective:** 22 August 2026

Welding Gas Wallet is a recordkeeping utility. It does not inspect, certify, authenticate, approve, or determine the real-world condition or legal status of any cylinder.

## The App does not determine

- cylinder ownership, title, rental/lease rights, deposit rights, or right of possession;
- whether a cylinder is safe, damaged, contaminated, compatible, within test/inspection date, or otherwise suitable for service;
- whether a cylinder may legally or contractually be filled, exchanged, transported, stored, returned, sold, or transferred;
- whether a gas is suitable for a process, material, regulator, valve, workplace, or person;
- whether a supplier, employer, insurer, regulator, carrier, manufacturer, or other third party will accept a cylinder or record;
- compliance with workplace, transport, fire, pressure-vessel, hazardous-material, occupational-safety, environmental, or other requirements.

## User-entered records are not proof

Nicknames, serial numbers, supplier details, dates, costs, relationship labels, reminders, notes, and other entries are supplied by the user. Their presence in the App is not verification by GoodUse Studios and must not be treated as a certificate, inspection record, title document, regulatory determination, or supplier approval.

## Verify real-world status independently

Before filling, exchanging, transporting, storing, connecting, using, returning, or otherwise handling a cylinder, rely on the cylinder’s authoritative markings and documentation and on qualified suppliers, manufacturers, workplace procedures, professionals, and regulators as appropriate.

Reminders are convenience notifications only. A missed, delayed, or incorrect reminder does not change any real-world inspection, return, rental, lease, safety, or legal obligation.

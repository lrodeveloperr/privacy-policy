# Welding Gas Wallet Safety and Scope Disclaimer

**Publisher:** GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada  
**Effective and last updated:** 21 August 2026

Welding Gas Wallet is an administrative record tool. Its output reflects facts
entered by the user. It is not a cylinder inspection, certification, ownership,
safety, compliance, engineering, welding, gas-selection, transport, storage,
legal, insurance, or supplier-acceptance service.

## No verification or verdict

The app does not independently inspect a cylinder or verify any label, serial,
photo, document, relationship selection, date, cost, supplier, or user entry.
“Owned,” “rented,” “leased,” “deposit,” “current,” and “returned” are record
labels selected by the user, not legal findings or proof.

The app does not determine whether a cylinder:

- is genuine, owned by the user, transferable, or lawfully possessed;
- is damaged, contaminated, leaking, empty, safe, or suitable for service;
- has a valid inspection, test, hydrostatic, or requalification status;
- may be filled, exchanged, transported, stored, connected, or used;
- contains the gas stated in an app record or is suitable for a process; or
- will be accepted by a supplier, employer, carrier, insurer, regulator, or
  other party.

## Use authoritative sources

An app record never overrides the physical cylinder label, current safety data,
manufacturer instructions, employer procedures, supplier requirements, or
applicable law. Obtain the appropriate determination from the relevant trained
person, supplier, manufacturer, employer, regulator, or emergency service.

Do not use an app reminder as the sole control for a safety, inspection,
requalification, return, contractual, or legal deadline. The operating system
can delay, suppress, or hide notifications.

## No gas or welding guidance

Gas descriptions and presets are record-entry conveniences only. The app does
not recommend a gas, mixture, process, pressure, connection, regulator,
procedure, or equipment configuration. User-entered cost and history data does
not establish that a refill, exchange, inspection, or other event actually
occurred or was proper.

## Emergencies

Do not rely on Welding Gas Wallet during a leak, fire, suspected damage,
exposure, or other urgent situation. Follow the applicable emergency procedure
and contact the appropriate trained or emergency service.

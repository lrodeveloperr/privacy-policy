# Safety and Legal Disclaimer

Welding Gas Wallet is a recordkeeping tool, not a welding-safety, inspection, certification or compliance authority.

It does not determine whether a cylinder is safe, fit for service, legally owned, correctly transported, correctly stored, correctly connected, suitable for a particular gas or process, or compliant with workplace, transport, pressure-vessel, environmental or other rules.

It does not scan cylinders, validate serial numbers, read pressure, track material certificates or consumable lots, or certify materials or personnel.

Cylinder status, reminders, costs, supplier information and history are personal records based on information entered by the user. They are not sensor readings or professional determinations.

Always follow the cylinder label, supplier instructions, applicable standards and law, and qualified professional or competent-person guidance where required.

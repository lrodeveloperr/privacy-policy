# Welding Gas Wallet store listing package

This page records the code-grounded English listing and disclosure baseline for the 3 September 2026 cylinder-only, no-ads release.

## App Store

**Name:** Welding Gas Wallet  
**Subtitle:** Cylinder inventory & reminders  
**Promotional text:** Know which welding-gas cylinders you have, what they cost and what needs attention next—offline, with quick status updates and optional reminders.  
**Keywords:** welding,gas,cylinder,bottle,inventory,refill,rental,oxygen,argon,acetylene,reminder,cost

### Description

Keep your welding-gas cylinders organised without spreadsheets or an account.

Welding Gas Wallet gives you one clear place to:

- Record gas type, capacity, serial number, supplier and ownership
- Mark a cylinder Ready, Low, Empty or Away with one tap
- Log refills, exchanges, rental or lease costs, deposits and notes
- Review a simple activity history
- Set optional local reminders for refills, payments, returns and checks
- Back up and restore through the system file picker

Your core records stay on your device unless you export a backup. The app contains no ads or tracking SDK and does not scan cylinders, use the camera, read tank pressure or make safety or compliance decisions.

Free includes up to three current editable cylinders. A US$19.99 one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited cylinders on iOS. The App Store's localized purchase sheet shows the authoritative price before confirmation. This purchase is not a subscription and does not renew automatically.

## Reviewer notes

### Apple App Review information

1. **Physical-device recording:** Attach a recording from the submitted production build on a current physical iPhone. Begin at cold launch and show onboarding, adding a cylinder, one-tap status, refill/exchange history, reminders, backup, restore preview, deletion, the fourth-cylinder paywall, purchase product, restore purchases and legal links. No advertising or consent prompt exists. **Blocked until a signed production build and physical-device recording exist.**
2. **Test devices:** Enter the exact physical iPhone/iPad models and OS versions actually tested. Do not list simulators as physical tests. **Blocked until physical-device testing is complete.**
3. **Function and audience:** An offline-first cylinder inventory for adult welders, fabricators, workshop operators and people who own, rent or exchange welding-gas cylinders. It replaces scattered notes with status, supplier, cost, history and reminder records. It does not measure gas, scan a cylinder or provide safety/compliance decisions.
4. **Access:** No login, demo account, external hardware or sample file is required. Accept the onboarding disclaimer, add a cylinder manually, use the four status buttons, record a refill or exchange, then open Settings for reminders, backup/restore, deletion, privacy and purchases.
5. **External services:** Apple In-App Purchase for the iOS lifetime unlock; Google Play Billing for Android subscriptions; operating-system local notifications; and user-selected file providers for backup. No publisher authentication, advertising, AI, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or cylinder-record backend is used.
6. **Regions:** Core features are consistent worldwide. Store prices, taxes, purchase mechanisms, default currency and measurement-unit suggestions may vary by store and device settings.
7. **Regulated/protected material:** The app is a user-entered personal inventory and does not provide welding certification, engineering, inspection, compressed-gas compliance or regulated safety decisions. It contains no third-party standards text, certification marks or protected training content.
8. **In-app purchase:** Open Settings and tap Upgrade, or attempt to add a fourth current cylinder. iOS shows one non-consumable lifetime Pro product that unlocks unlimited cylinders. The product's title and localized price appear before purchase, with Restore purchases, Terms and Privacy links. Do not add an App Store promotional image for this IAP unless a unique product-specific image is created; the app icon must not be reused.

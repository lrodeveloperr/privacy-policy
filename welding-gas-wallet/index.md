## Product scope at a glance

The app records facts you enter. It does **not** verify ownership or identity,
judge cylinder condition or safety, determine inspection or requalification
status, decide fillability, recommend a gas, or predict whether a supplier or
authority will accept a cylinder. Follow the cylinder label and the current
requirements and procedures supplied by the manufacturer, employer, supplier,
regulator, or other qualified authority.

The first three current cylinder records can be editable without paid access.
Attempting to add a fourth current cylinder preserves the draft and presents
the applicable store purchase. Android offers auto-renewing monthly and annual
subscriptions; iOS offers a one-time non-consumable lifetime unlock. The price
and terms displayed by Google Play or the App Store are authoritative.

# Local Data Deletion for Welding Gas Wallet

**Last updated:** 22 August 2026

Welding Gas Wallet does not require a GoodUse Studios account and does not keep the user’s cylinder wallet in a GoodUse Studios cloud database. Local App data is therefore deleted on the device rather than through an online account-deletion request.

## Delete all App-managed wallet data

In Welding Gas Wallet, open **Settings**, choose **Delete all local data**, review the confirmation, and confirm deletion.

The App is designed to cancel App-managed reminders first and then remove the local wallet data it manages, including cylinder records, event/history records, supplier records, reminders, pending drafts, settings stored with the wallet, and App-managed recovery copies. If reminder cancellation fails, deletion fails closed so the user can retry rather than leaving untraceable scheduled notifications.

## Uninstalling

Uninstalling the App removes its app-private local storage according to the device operating system. On Android, the App build disables Android application backup, so Welding Gas Wallet does not opt its private wallet file into Android cloud backup/device-transfer backup.

## What local deletion does not delete

Local deletion cannot erase:

- backup files the user previously exported or shared;
- copies stored by a file provider, email provider, cloud drive, recipient, or another device;
- Google Play or Apple purchase/accounting records controlled by those stores;
- support emails or other information the user deliberately sent to GoodUse Studios;
- information independently held by a supplier, employer, regulator, insurer, manufacturer, or other third party.

Delete exported copies at every destination where they were saved or shared. Store-account information must be managed through the applicable store.

## Information held by GoodUse Studios

For information actually sent to or otherwise held by GoodUse Studios, email **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com** with a privacy or deletion request. GoodUse Studios cannot search for or delete local cylinder records it never received.

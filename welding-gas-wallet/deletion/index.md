# Welding Gas Wallet local data deletion and no-account explanation

**Publisher:** GoodUse Studios, Ontario, Canada  
**Effective and last updated:** 21 August 2026

## No GoodUse Studios account

Welding Gas Wallet does not require or create a GoodUse Studios account. Its
cylinder records are stored on the device by default, not in a GoodUse Studios
cylinder-record server. There is therefore normally no remote Welding Gas
Wallet account or server-side cylinder wallet for GoodUse Studios to delete on
request.

## Delete one cylinder record

Open the cylinder's **More** actions, choose **Delete cylinder**, review the
warning, and confirm. This removes the selected local record and its app-managed
history and reminder from the active wallet. A local recovery snapshot may
temporarily contain the previous safe wallet until later writes replace it or
the app's local storage is removed.

## Delete all local app data

To remove all app-managed local data, use an in-app **Delete all local data**
control if the installed version provides one. Otherwise:

- on Android, clear the app's storage/data in system settings or uninstall it;
- on iOS, choose **Delete App**, not **Offload App**, when the intention is to
  remove local app data.

Platform behavior and device backups can vary. Confirm the applicable device
instructions before relying on deletion.

## Delete exports and backups separately

Deleting local app data does not remove copies you exported, shared, emailed,
printed, transferred, or placed in device or cloud backup. Delete each copy
from its destination and, where appropriate, its trash or recovery folder.
Recipients control copies already sent to them.

## Purchases are separate

Deleting records, clearing data, or uninstalling does not cancel an Android
subscription, erase store purchase history, or revoke an iOS non-consumable
purchase. Manage Android renewal through Google Play. Use the App Store's
purchase and refund tools for iOS purchases.

Email [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com) and identify

record content that GoodUse Studios does not possess.

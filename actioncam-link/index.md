## Product and purchase boundary

The app has no developer account, advertising, analytics, tracking, developer backend, or developer cloud. It communicates with compatible cameras at private local addresses and stores downloads on the Apple device until the user exports or deletes them.

The free, opt-in **1-day Trial** lasts 24 hours from its verified App Store transaction. It does not renew and causes no automatic charge. A separate **Lifetime Unlock** is a one-time non-consumable purchase. There is no subscription.

<p class="notice"><strong>Compatibility:</strong> Exact hardware and firmware determine which features work. Test connection and preview before starting the trial or purchasing. ActionCam Link is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any camera manufacturer.</p>

**Effective:** September 8, 2026

- **1-day Trial:** Free Price Tier 0 non-consumable. It begins only after you choose to start it and Apple returns a verified transaction.
- **Duration:** Exactly 24 hours from the verified transaction purchase time.
- **During the trial:** Recording, supported photo capture, new downloads, and camera-original deletion are unlocked on compatible cameras.
- **After the trial:** Those features lock. Connection checks, supported preview, media browsing, demo mode, and existing on-device downloads remain available.
- **Renewal and charge:** The trial does not renew and causes no automatic charge.
- **Continued access:** Requires a separate Lifetime Unlock non-consumable at the localized App Store price shown before purchase.
- **Restore:** Restore Purchases checks both non-consumable transactions for the current Apple Account.
- **Pending, cancelled, unverified, refunded, or revoked:** Access is not granted until StoreKit returns a verified, non-revoked entitlement. Revoked lifetime access is removed.

Apple processes all transactions and controls payment, refund, and Apple Account purchase-history services.

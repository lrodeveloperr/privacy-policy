# CNC Repeat Job Bench — Privacy Policy

This policy concerns the **CNC Repeat Job Bench iPhone and iPad app**. The app helps an operator record repeat CNC setups, measurements, production counts, inspections, issues, handoffs and job history. It is designed to work without an app account or a WorksBien-hosted shop database.

## Data in the app

You or other users of the device may enter names, part and work-order identifiers, machine and drawing references, dimensions and observed readings, notes, counts, audit reasons, handoff details, and optional photo or PDF attachments. The app stores this information in its local database on the device. The operator name is also stored in the app's local preferences; purchase status may be cached in the device's Keychain. Data needed for exports and archives is stored in local app files. Names, images, drawings and notes may contain personal or confidential business information if you put it there.

The app uses this data to display the workflow, compare entered readings against entered tolerances, apply inspection and hold rules, produce a run history, and prepare files that you choose to export. It does not connect to a CNC control or independently observe, measure or certify a workpiece.

The current ad-free iOS app has no developer-operated account, analytics feed, advertising or built-in shop-data synchronization. We will update this policy if the distributed app gains a service or data practice that changes this description.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are offered through Apple's StoreKit. The app checks Apple-verified entitlement information to decide whether additional part-and-machine families may be approved or started. Apple handles the purchase and payment flow under its own terms. The app does not request your payment-card number. Apple may provide the developer with transaction and sales information through App Store Connect; Apple's handling of your Apple Account and billing information is governed by Apple's policies.

## Sharing, backup and other recipients

Nothing in the normal job-entry flow uploads your shop records to WorksBien. **You decide** whether to create a CSV, PDF or JSON file, use the iOS share sheet, or send an archive/backup elsewhere. The destination you select may store or disclose the file under its own rules. Check that the recipient is authorized to see any job, employee or customer information before sharing.

The app's database and archives may be included in an iOS device backup if you enable the relevant Apple backup settings. This is different from an in-app synchronization service. You can manage Apple backup settings and backups through your device and Apple Account. Local JSON backups and exported archive files remain wherever you save or share them; deleting the app does not delete copies held by other people or services.

If you email support, we receive your email address and what you choose to include in the message or attachment. Please avoid sending confidential drawings or measurements unless needed for your request and you are authorized to share them. We use support messages to respond, investigate the request, and meet applicable record obligations. We do not access the on-device job database merely because you contact us.

## Security, retention and deletion

The app writes its database, archives and prepared exports with iOS file-protection options and validates backup/archive data before restore. No app can guarantee that a lost, compromised, backed-up or deliberately shared file remains confidential. Protect access to your device and manage exported files as shop records.

Job records remain on the device until you remove the app or replace/restore the local database; an archive operation moves completed runs to a separate local file and retains a receipt in the database. To remove local app data, use iOS to delete the app and separately remove any exported files and device or cloud backups you control. Restoring a backup may put prior records back on the device. We cannot directly delete files stored only on your device. Contact us to ask about support correspondence we hold; we will assess and respond to requests under applicable law. Support correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and applicable legal obligations.

## Children and changes

This app is designed for shop and professional workflows, not directed to children. If the app's behavior or this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the effective date and, when required, notify users or obtain consent.

# CNC Repeat Job Bench — Legal and Support

**Operator:** WorksBien Studios Inc., Ontario, Canada  
**Effective date:** 24 September 2026  
**Contact:** [info@worksbienstudios.com](mailto:info@worksbienstudios.com)

CNC Repeat Job Bench is a local-first iPhone and iPad workflow tool for repeat CNC setups, first-piece reviews, in-process inspections and run history. The first two distinct part-and-machine families are free; monthly or annual Pro permits additional families while active. It does not connect to a CNC machine, measure parts, certify conformity or replace shop safety procedures.

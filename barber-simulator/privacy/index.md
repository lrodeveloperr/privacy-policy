# Privacy Policy for Barber Simulator: ASMR Haircut

**Effective:** 10 November 2026  
**Applies to:** Barber Simulator: ASMR Haircut 1.0 for Windows and macOS

WorksBien Studios Inc. ("WorksBien Studios," "we," "us," or "our") publishes Barber Simulator: ASMR Haircut (the "Game"). This policy explains what the Game processes, what stays on the device, and what platform providers may process separately.

## 1. Plain-language summary

- The Game has no WorksBien account, advertising, behavioural analytics, tracking, developer-operated cloud service, or third-party crash-reporting SDK.
- WorksBien Studios does not receive gameplay, player-created salon names, settings, progress, haircut results, or save files during ordinary play.
- Game progress and settings are stored locally on the player's device.
- Microsoft, Apple, or Valve may separately process store transactions, licensing, platform diagnostics, or optional platform services under their own terms and privacy policies.
- If a player contacts support, WorksBien Studios receives the email address and information the player chooses to send.

## 2. Information stored locally

The Game stores progress, earned coins, unlocked designs, settings, graphics measurements, career progress, customer history, and player-entered salon text in local application storage. Desktop editions may also create rotating local backup files so the player can restore progress.

This information stays on the device unless the player chooses a platform backup or cloud-save feature that is available for that edition. WorksBien Studios does not operate a server that receives these saves.

Local information remains until the player deletes it through available game or operating-system controls, removes backup files, uninstalls the Game subject to platform backup behaviour, or erases the device.

## 3. Microsoft Store and operating-system services

Microsoft processes Microsoft Store acquisition, licensing, payment, refund, tax, security, device, and diagnostic information under Microsoft's terms, privacy statement, account settings, and Windows diagnostic settings. WorksBien Studios does not receive full payment-card or bank details.

Windows may include eligible local Game data in user-controlled device or OneDrive backups according to Windows settings. Apple may similarly process Mac App Store transactions or platform diagnostics for a macOS edition.

## 4. Steam edition and optional Steam Cloud

The desktop shell can support Steamworks when a Steam edition is launched through Steam with a valid Steam app ID and a running Steam client. In that situation, Valve may process Steam account, entitlement, overlay, diagnostic, and optional Steam Cloud information under Valve's terms and privacy policy. An optional Steam Cloud save is handled by Steam; it is not uploaded to a WorksBien-operated server.

The Microsoft Store edition does not require a Steam account or Steam Cloud for ordinary play.

## 5. Diagnostics and support

The Game contains no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK and does not send gameplay analytics to WorksBien Studios. Microsoft, Apple, Valve, the operating system, or device manufacturer may provide platform diagnostics or aggregate business reports according to their services and the player's settings.

If a player emails support, WorksBien Studios receives the email address and message content the player chooses to provide. Players should send only the Game version, platform, operating-system version, a neutral description of the issue, and non-sensitive reproduction steps. Do not send passwords, payment-card information, private identifiers, or confidential files.

## 6. Sharing, sale, tracking, and retention

WorksBien Studios does not receive, sell, rent, or use local Game information for advertising or cross-app tracking. We may retain support correspondence and platform-issued business records only as reasonably needed for support, security, legal, tax, accounting, or dispute purposes.

Microsoft, Apple, Valve, GitHub Pages, email providers, network providers, operating-system vendors, and device manufacturers are independent providers for their own services.

## 7. Privacy requests, children, and security

WorksBien Studios cannot access, correct, export, or delete local Game information that it never receives. Players can use the Game's save controls, local backup controls, platform cloud-save controls, operating-system storage controls, or uninstall process as applicable.

For information actually held by WorksBien Studios, contact **info@worksbienstudios.com**. We may need to verify identity or authority before acting on a request.

The Game is not designed to collect children's personal information or transmit it to WorksBien Studios. No device, platform, or storage system is completely secure; players should protect their devices and platform accounts with appropriate access controls.

## 8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the Game, law, or data practices change. The current version is published at this URL with its effective date. Material changes will receive an appropriate Game or store notice where required.

Operator and privacy contact: **WorksBien Studios Inc.**, **info@worksbienstudios.com**. Customer service: [worksbienstudios.com/customerservice](https://worksbienstudios.com/customerservice).

# Support and Data Deletion for SupplyCheck Local

**Last updated:** 10 August 2026

## Support

Email: **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**

For faster support, include:

- Android or Apple;
- device model and operating-system version;
- SupplyCheck Local version;
- the screen and steps involved; and
- the exact error text.

For purchase support, include only a redacted store order or transaction reference. Never send payment-card details, passwords, PINs, confidential supplier documents or personal information that is not necessary. Redact supplier names, invoice references and prices from screenshots where possible.

SupplyCheck Local has no account and no GoodUse Studios cloud database containing your delivery records. We cannot view, retrieve, edit or restore records stored only on your device.

## Delete local App data

### Android

To delete all SupplyCheck Local operational data while keeping the App installed, use the device's **Settings > Apps > SupplyCheck Local > Storage > Clear storage/data** control. Menu wording varies by device.

Deleting the App also deletes its Android app storage. The production Android configuration disables application backup. Generated reports in the App's private cache are removed when App storage is cleared or the App is deleted, but copies you exported or sent are not.

### Apple

Delete SupplyCheck Local from the device to delete its local App container. Do not choose **Offload App** if your goal is deletion, because offloading may preserve documents and data for later reinstallation.

Apple device backups may contain App data under your Apple backup settings. Manage or delete those backups through Apple device or account settings. GoodUse Studios cannot access or delete an Apple-controlled backup.

### Exported or shared reports

A PDF saved to Files, email, messaging, cloud storage or another app is outside SupplyCheck Local. Delete each external copy from the destination and, where relevant, from its trash, deleted-items folder, synced devices and backups. Ask recipients to delete their copies where appropriate.

## Delete information held by GoodUse Studios

The App itself does not send operational records to us. If you previously emailed support, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information in that correspondence by emailing lrodeveloperr@gmail.com from the same address where possible.

We may retain limited information when required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, security, an unresolved dispute, or another legal obligation. Deleting support correspondence does not delete store transaction records held by Apple or Google; contact the relevant Store regarding its records.

## Purchases after local-data deletion

Clearing storage or deleting the App does not automatically cancel an Android subscription. Cancel through Google Play if you do not want future renewal.

An eligible paid entitlement may be restored through the same Store account after reinstalling the production App. Restoration does not restore supplier, delivery, discrepancy, recovery or PDF records.

# Support and Data Deletion for SupplyCheck Local

**Document version:** 2026-08-19  
**Effective:** when this version is first published and linked from the released App  
**Applies to:** SupplyCheck Local 1.4.0 (build 140) for iPhone/iOS

This page explains safe support and local deletion for **SupplyCheck Local (displayed on the device as SupplyCheck)**, provided by Lateef Razaq-Oyetola carrying on business as GoodUse Studios.

## 1. Contact and support boundary

Email: **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**  
Postal address: 36 Zorra Street, Toronto (Etobicoke), Ontario M8Z 0G5, Canada

Approved neutral support information:

- iPhone model and iOS version;
- SupplyCheck version/build and schema version;
- selected locale;
- product ID and transaction phase for a purchase issue;
- neutral error code or non-content error text; and
- a concise description of the screen/action without supplier, person, item, amount, reference, or file content.

Do **not** send screenshots, orders, invoices, supplier/employee names, contact details, prices, notes, photos, source documents, PDFs, spreadsheets, readable exports, databases, backup/move packages, control proofs, payment-card details, passwords, PINs, or recovery material. Ordinary support does not accept operational records or images.

SupplyCheck has no customer account and no GoodUse operational-data cloud. GoodUse Studios cannot view, retrieve, edit, restore, or remotely delete records stored only on a device.

## 2. Export before deletion

If a lawful copy is needed, use **Export all organisation data (readable)** or create a SupplyCheck machine backup before deletion. Preview the scope. Outward files are not password-protected by SupplyCheck and must be protected and later deleted at the selected destination.

Export is optional and does not cancel a pending-deletion deadline. Do not email the result to GoodUse Studios.

## 3. Delete one record

Open the record's detail/settings actions, review the exact scope, and choose the permanent-delete action. Confirm when prompted and keep the App open until the journal completes. The App removes the record's content-bearing revisions, notes, attachments/photos, retained originals, temporary derivatives, and exact marked-shared PDF bytes from its private storage, while retaining only the permitted content-free tombstone/evaluation continuity needed to prevent resurrection and allowance reset.

## 4. Delete all organisation data

Open Settings → Data & Continuity → **Delete all organisation data**. Review the preview of records, drafts, attachments, recoveries, retained originals, reports, backup/outward-copy observations, unresolved identity families, and external-copy limitations. Complete native device authentication where configured; use the warned strong-confirmation fallback only if the operating system reports authentication unsupported or unconfigured before prompting.

The action terminalizes unresolved identity families, fences late callbacks, and removes content-bearing local derivatives before reporting success. Interrupted post-commit cleanup resumes before ordinary use. Legal presentation/acceptance records are operational local metadata and are purged, so a later organisation receives the current Privacy Policy and accepts the current Terms again.

Delete All may retain only the disclosed bounded, opaque, content-free evaluation-claim/exhaustion, identity-equivalence/terminal-family, and destruction-generation continuity needed to prevent allowance reset, stale callback recreation, or old-package resurrection. It does not retain readable organisation, supplier, person, delivery, date, amount, note, or attachment content.

## 5. Remove the App or its storage

Deleting the App or clearing its local storage removes App-contained local content according to iOS storage behaviour. It does not delete copies already exported, shared, saved to another provider/device, or retained in customer-controlled backups, and it does not erase Apple's own purchase or diagnostic records.

Reinstalling SupplyCheck does not recreate operational records that GoodUse Studios never received. An eligible Pro entitlement may be re-queried separately through **Restore Purchases**. We do not claim forensic erasure of underlying device media.

## 6. External and older copies

SupplyCheck cannot delete a PDF, spreadsheet, readable export, backup, copy, or move package after it is handed to another app, person, device, or provider. Delete those copies at every destination. An old isolated backup restored without knowledge of a later deletion can reintroduce content; a restore/merge suppresses deleted content only when matching deletion-continuity information is available. We do not claim deleted everywhere, remote wipe, or forensic erasure.

## 7. Automatic local retention

The local retention design is:

- temporary import/report/share files expire within 24 hours plus purge on the next execution after expiry;
- inactive Drafts and unattached staging enter seven-day Pending deletion after 90 days;
- completed records use 1-year, 3-year, 7-year, or Manual-deletion retention selected by the organisation/record;
- fixed retention runs from the latest substantive revision or, for recovery, the later substantive revision and resolution/closure;
- open recovery and unresolved conflicts pause automatic deletion; and
- photos, retained originals, attachments, and marked-shared PDF bytes inherit the parent record's retention.

## 8. Privacy requests concerning GoodUse-held data

For support, privacy, or security correspondence actually held by GoodUse Studios, email **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com** from the same address where practical. State the requested access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other right without attaching operational records. We may verify identity/authority and retain limited information where required for legal, tax, security, incident, or unresolved-dispute purposes.

For operational records held only by a customer organisation, contact that organisation and use its local SupplyCheck tools. GoodUse Studios cannot search or delete information it never received. Apple controls its own purchase/diagnostic records and provides its own rights/support processes.

## 9. Security reports

Email **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com** with the affected version, a non-sensitive description, and safe reproduction steps. Do not include live business content, exploit personal data, credentials, or destructive payloads. GoodUse Studios will triage the report and may provide a safer follow-up channel.

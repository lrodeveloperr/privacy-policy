# Privacy Policy for SupplyCheck

**Effective:** 22 August 2026  
**Applies to:** SupplyCheck 2.1.0 on Android and SupplyCheck 2.0.0 on iPhone/iOS

Lateef Razaq-Oyetola carrying on business as GoodUse Studios ("GoodUse Studios," "we," "us," or "our") publishes SupplyCheck (the "App"). This policy explains what the App processes, what normally stays on the device, and what may leave it. Platform-specific differences are identified below.

## 1. Plain-language summary

- SupplyCheck works without a GoodUse Studios account or inventory cloud.
- GoodUse Studios does not receive the stock records created during ordinary app use.
- **Android 2.1.0:** the full App is available without a feature paywall. Unless an active monthly ad-free subscription is verified, a permanent bottom banner may display **non-personalized ads** through Google Mobile Ads. Google User Messaging Platform is used for applicable consent/privacy choices, and Google Play Billing is used for the optional ad-free subscription.
- **iPhone/iOS 2.0.0:** there is no advertising SDK or SupplyCheck subscription in this version; access remains through the App Store purchase model applicable to that version.
- SupplyCheck does not include behavioural analytics or a third-party crash-reporting SDK.
- Data also leaves the App when the user deliberately exports or shares a backup or CSV file, contacts support, or uses operating-system backup/device-transfer features.

## 2. Information processed locally

Depending on how the App is used, local information may include workspace and location names or details; supplier names; item names, SKUs, units, and minimum levels; physical counts; receipts, use, and loss movements; timestamps; optional notes and correction reasons; archived items or locations; and versioned backup or CSV-export data. Language, appearance, text-size, and accessibility choices are selected through operating-system settings rather than stored as a SupplyCheck profile.

This information may identify people or reveal confidential business operations if the user enters names or sensitive notes. Only enter information the organisation is authorized to use and minimize personal information.

**Inventory content is not intentionally included in advertising or subscription requests.** The Android advertising and billing components operate separately from the local inventory ledger.

## 3. Why local information is processed

The App processes local information to display stock by location, distinguish an unknown balance from a counted zero, calculate projected balances, identify low or out-of-stock items, preserve an append-only movement history, create corrections as new physical counts, manage items/locations/suppliers, and create user-requested backups and exports.

## 4. Android advertising and privacy choices

Android 2.1.0 uses Google Mobile Ads to display a standard banner at the bottom of the App when no active ad-free subscription has been verified. SupplyCheck requests the banner as **non-personalized advertising**. Non-personalized does not mean that no technical data is processed: Google and participating advertising providers may process information needed to request, deliver, secure, measure, limit, and report ads, such as IP/network information, device or app information, advertising-related identifiers where permitted, consent/privacy signals, and ad interaction or diagnostic information, subject to Google’s policies, user settings, applicable law, and the consent choices presented.

SupplyCheck uses Google User Messaging Platform to request current consent information and display a consent form or privacy-options entry where required. The App does not request a banner ad until the platform indicates that ads may be requested. SupplyCheck additionally marks each banner request as non-personalized.

When Google Play confirms an active SupplyCheck ad-free monthly subscription, the Android App removes the banner and does not make banner-ad requests while that verified entitlement is active. Google Play may still be contacted to verify subscription status. If entitlement cannot be verified, the App does not assume that an ad-free entitlement exists.

Google’s own privacy controls and notices apply to Google Mobile Ads, User Messaging Platform, and Google Play. GoodUse Studios does not receive the user’s full payment-card or bank details.

## 5. Android ad-free subscription

The optional Android subscription is used only to remove the permanent banner; it does not unlock inventory features. Google Play processes purchase, renewal, cancellation, refund, tax, currency, account, and entitlement information under Google’s terms and privacy notices. The App receives limited purchase state and product/entitlement information from Google Play so it can determine whether to hide the banner and acknowledge a completed purchase.

The intended United States list price is **US$2.99 per month**, but Google Play may display a localized price, currency, tax treatment, offer, or equivalent amount. The price shown by Google Play at checkout controls the transaction.

## 6. Storage, backups, and exports

Live inventory records are stored in app-private local storage. SupplyCheck does not provide app-level inventory cloud synchronization.

- **Android:** the private inventory file may participate in Android's operating-system-managed cloud backup or device transfer, depending on the device, account, and backup settings. Platform protections apply; SupplyCheck adds no separate backup password or encryption layer.
- **iPhone/iOS:** Application Support data may participate in an Apple device, Finder, or iCloud device backup according to Apple's platform behaviour and the user's settings.
- **Exports:** files deliberately saved or shared by the user are controlled by the selected recipient, app, file provider, cloud service, or device. SupplyCheck does not add a separate password or encryption layer to exported JSON or CSV files.

GoodUse Studios cannot retrieve, synchronize, remotely wipe, or restore inventory records it never received. Protect the device with a passcode, restrict device access, and store exports only in approved destinations.

## 7. Other information GoodUse Studios or platforms may receive

**Store transactions.** Apple or Google processes store purchases and subscriptions. GoodUse Studios does not receive full card or bank details. Store dashboards may provide transaction, storefront, refund, sales, tax, subscription-status, and aggregate performance information. Depending on the platform and a specific support or refund workflow, the store may expose a limited order identifier or account/contact field controlled by the store.

**Platform diagnostics.** Apple or Google may provide platform crash or diagnostic information according to device and developer-console settings. SupplyCheck does not intentionally add inventory content to diagnostic messages.

**Beta and testing feedback.** Apple, Google, or another authorized testing platform may make tester feedback, screenshots, account/contact details, device/build information, and crash or diagnostic data available to GoodUse Studios under the platform's testing terms and tester choices. Testers must not include stock records, screenshots of live inventory, exports, backups, or other confidential business data in feedback.

**Support and privacy correspondence.** If someone emails us, we receive the address and information they choose to send. Please provide only the App version/build, device and operating-system version, selected locale, a neutral error message, and steps that do not contain business records. Do not send inventory exports, backups, customer or employee data, credentials, or payment-card information.

**Policy website.** These pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub and network providers may process request, IP-address, browser/device, cookie, security, and log information under their own notices.

## 8. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell local stock data and cannot share inventory data we do not receive. On Android, Google and advertising providers may process advertising-related information as described above; Google Play processes subscription information. When a user deliberately exports or shares a file, the selected destination processes its copy under its own terms. Apple, Google, device-backup providers, email providers, GitHub, and selected export destinations are independent providers for their respective services.

We may disclose information actually in our possession when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or security, investigate misuse, or establish or defend legal claims.

## 9. Retention and deletion

Local operational records remain in app-private storage until changed, the workspace is reset, app data is cleared or the App is uninstalled, or the operating system removes the storage. Archiving hides an item or location from active work but preserves it and its related history. On iPhone, **Settings → Backup and restore → Reset to setup** deletes the current workspace, items, locations, suppliers, movement history, and recovery/safety files held by the App on that device after confirmation. On Android, use **Settings → Reset → Reset workspace**.

Neither action deletes files already exported or copies held in device/cloud backups. Those copies must be deleted at each destination. Store, advertising, consent, and subscription information held by Google or Apple is governed by those providers’ retention rules and user controls. Support correspondence and store records held by GoodUse Studios are retained only as reasonably needed for support, security, legal, tax, accounting, or dispute purposes.

## 10. Rights and requests

Use the App's local tools for records held only on the device. GoodUse Studios cannot search or delete information it never received. For information actually held by GoodUse Studios, email the Privacy Lead at **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com** to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or another applicable right. We may verify identity and authority. Apple, Google, GitHub, advertising providers, and selected export providers handle requests for information they control.

To challenge our privacy compliance, email **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com** with the subject **Privacy complaint** and explain the concern without attaching operational records. The Privacy Lead will investigate and respond within a reasonable period appropriate to the request and applicable law. If the concern is not resolved, you may contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/report-a-concern/) or another regulator with lawful jurisdiction.

## 11. Children, international use, and security

SupplyCheck is a business utility and is not directed to children. No system is completely secure. Device loss, hardware failure, reset, uninstall, storage corruption, or an incompatible update can make local data unavailable. Users are responsible for appropriate access controls, lawful data entry, backup choices, and destination security.

For support correspondence, store information, or testing feedback that GoodUse Studios actually receives, we limit access to accounts and people who need it, use available platform account-security controls, and delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably needed, subject to legal, tax, accounting, security, and dispute obligations. Email and third-party platforms carry their own security risks, so do not send operational records or other sensitive content.

## 12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the App, law, or data practices change. The current version is published at this URL with its effective date. Material changes will be presented through an appropriate app or store notice where required.

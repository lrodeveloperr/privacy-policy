# Operational Disclaimer for SupplyCheck Local

**Last updated:** 10 August 2026

SupplyCheck Local is an operational comparison and recordkeeping aid. It works only with information entered and confirmed by the user.

## Not an authoritative system or professional opinion

The App and its reports are not:

- a purchase order, invoice, credit note, acceptance certificate or supplier statement;
- an inventory, accounting, tax, audit, legal or regulatory system;
- food-safety, product-safety, quality-control or inspection advice;
- a determination of contractual rights, liability, fault, fraud or supplier misconduct;
- proof that goods were ordered, invoiced, received, damaged, returned or credited; or
- a guarantee that a supplier will accept a discrepancy, issue a credit, replace goods or pay any amount.

Original contracts, purchase orders, invoices, delivery documents, credit notes, product labels, physical inspection and direct supplier confirmation remain authoritative. Obtain qualified accounting, tax, legal, regulatory or safety advice where needed.

## User-entered calculations

The App normalizes quantities using the pack sizes and price bases entered by the user. Its estimated billed exposure may include invoiced units not received, billed unordered units, billed damaged units and price increases on usable ordered units. The calculation can be wrong or incomplete if an input, unit, pack size, price basis, tax, discount, surcharge, allowance, invoice status or other relevant term is missing or incorrect.

An invoice-pending result is provisional. A price-change tolerance is a display and calculation preference, not a statement about contractual acceptability or fair market price.

## Decisions remain with the user

You must independently verify every discrepancy and report before using it. You decide whether goods should be accepted, rejected, stored, used, paid for, returned or disputed. Do not rely on the App for a decision that could affect health, safety, legal compliance or urgent business continuity.

Recording "Credit promised," "Credit received," "Replacement received" or another recovery event reflects only the user's entry. The App does not verify the supplier's agreement or the value recovered.

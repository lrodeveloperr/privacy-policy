# Privacy Policy for SupplyCheck Local

**Effective date:** 10 August 2026  
**Last updated:** 10 August 2026

GoodUse Studios ("GoodUse Studios," "we," "us," or "our") publishes SupplyCheck Local (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information and how we handle information you choose to send us for support.

## 1. Privacy summary

SupplyCheck Local is designed so that your supplier, delivery, pricing, discrepancy and recovery records remain in the App's local storage on your device. The App does not require an account and does not use a GoodUse Studios cloud database. We do not receive your operational records unless you deliberately send them to us, for example in a support email.

The App contains no advertising, behavioural tracking, developer-operated analytics, remote OCR, supplier connection or remote AI service.

When store purchases are enabled, Apple or Google processes the purchase. Store billing is the only planned network-dependent App function. Store billing does not transmit your supplier, invoice, item, price, discrepancy, report or recovery records to GoodUse Studios.

## 2. Information stored locally by the App

Depending on what you enter or create, local App data may include:

- supplier names;
- order, purchase-order, invoice, credit-note or other references;
- item names, optional SKU or barcode text, quantities, pack sizes and prices;
- currency and price-change tolerance;
- substitution, damage and discrepancy notes;
- check dates, duration and completion status;
- calculated discrepancy flags and estimated billed exposure;
- supplier-contact, promised-credit, credit-received, replacement, note and follow-up entries;
- generated PDF report content;
- appearance and other App settings; and
- optional, local-only validation feedback about willingness to pay.

This information is stored in the App's sandboxed Room database and preferences on Android or SwiftData and App preferences on Apple devices. It is used only to provide the App's functions on your device.

## 3. Information the App does not collect for GoodUse Studios

The App does not transmit the locally stored operational data listed above to us. The App does not ask for or collect, for us:

- an App account, password or profile;
- payment-card or bank-account details;
- precise or approximate device location;
- contacts, microphone recordings or call data;
- advertising identifiers;
- browsing history;
- health or biometric information; or
- data for advertising, cross-app tracking or sale to data brokers.

The production build must be re-evaluated before release if any permission, third-party SDK, advertising, analytics, cloud sync, photo capture, barcode scanning, OCR, backup/import or remote service is added.

## 4. App-store purchases

If you buy or restore a paid product, Apple App Store or Google Play processes the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details. The Store and the App may process limited purchase information needed to complete and maintain the entitlement, such as the product purchased, transaction or order identifier, purchase status, renewal or expiry status, refund or revocation status, and storefront or country. Stores may also provide us with sales, tax and financial reports.

We use purchase information only to provide or restore paid access, respond to purchase-support requests, prevent fraud, keep required business records, and comply with law. See the [Purchase and Subscription Terms](PURCHASE-AND-SUBSCRIPTION-TERMS.md) and [Refund and Cancellation Policy](REFUND-AND-CANCELLATION-POLICY.md).

Apple and Google's practices are governed by their own policies. You should review the privacy information presented by the Store before purchasing.

## 5. PDF export and sharing

PDF reports are generated on your device. On Android, a generated report is placed in the App's private cache before the system share sheet opens. On Apple devices, a report is placed in the App's temporary directory before the system share sheet opens.

The App sends a report only to the destination you select through the operating system's share sheet. Once you share or save a report outside the App, the selected recipient, app, storage provider or service controls that copy under its own terms and privacy practices. Deleting the App does not delete copies you previously exported or sent.

## 6. Device backups and security

The App does not operate a cloud-sync or developer backup service. Android's production manifest disables Android application backup. Apple device or operating-system backup settings may nevertheless include App data in a device backup controlled by Apple and by you. Exported PDFs may also be backed up by the destination you selected.

Local data is protected by the mobile operating system's app sandbox and by your device security. The App does not claim to provide independent end-to-end encryption. You should use a device passcode, keep the operating system current, restrict access to the device, and avoid entering unnecessary confidential or personal information.

No storage method is infallible. Loss, damage, reset, uninstall, storage failure or an incompatible update may cause permanent data loss. GoodUse Studios cannot retrieve App records from your device.

## 7. Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, device or App version, screenshots and purchase reference. Do not send payment-card information, passwords, confidential supplier documents, or personal information that is not necessary to resolve the issue. Redact supplier and invoice information wherever possible.

We use support information to answer your request, troubleshoot, protect the App, handle a purchase issue, and keep records reasonably required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes. Support email is processed by our email provider and may be stored outside your country.

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes and then delete or de-identify it, subject to legal retention duties and the need to preserve records relating to an unresolved dispute or security matter.

## 8. Sale, sharing, advertising and analytics

GoodUse Studios does not sell or rent personal information. The App does not share operational records with advertising networks, does not contain ads and does not use developer-operated behavioural analytics. We do not use App data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You control the operational data in the App because it remains on your device. You may stop entering data, export a PDF, clear the App's storage where your operating system permits, or delete the App. Instructions are in [Support and Data Deletion](SUPPORT-AND-DATA-DELETION.md).

For personal information that GoodUse Studios actually holds, such as a support email, you may ask us to provide access, correct it, or delete it, and you may withdraw consent or object to certain processing where applicable law provides that right. We may need to retain limited information when required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Contact us at lrodeveloperr@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request before acting.

You may also have the right to complain to the privacy regulator in your jurisdiction. These rights do not apply to information held only on your device, because we cannot access or identify it.

## 10. International processing

GoodUse Studios is based in Ontario, Canada. Support providers, Apple, Google and other services you independently choose may process information in Canada, the United States or other countries. Their laws may differ from those where you live. Store and third-party processing is governed by their own policies and lawful transfer mechanisms.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. A person who cannot legally agree to the Terms of Use must not purchase or use the App without the involvement of a parent, guardian or other authorized adult.

We may use or disclose information we actually possess when reasonably necessary to comply with law, a valid legal process, or enforceable governmental request; protect rights, safety and security; investigate fraud or abuse; or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

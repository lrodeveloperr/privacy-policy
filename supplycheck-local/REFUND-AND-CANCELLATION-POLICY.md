# Refund and Cancellation Policy for SupplyCheck Local

**Effective date:** 10 August 2026  
**Last updated:** 10 August 2026

SupplyCheck Local purchases are processed by the store from which you obtained the App. GoodUse Studios does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. Store rules and mandatory consumer law determine refund eligibility.

## 1. Android subscriptions

### Cancel future renewal

You may cancel an Android subscription through Google Play:

1. Open Google Play's **Payments & subscriptions** or visit [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).
2. Select SupplyCheck Local.
3. Choose **Cancel subscription** and follow Google's instructions.

Uninstalling or deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the billing period already paid, so paid capacity normally continues until then. Account hold, refund, revocation, chargeback or other store action may end access earlier. Google Play controls the renewal date and cancellation status shown in your account.

### Request a refund

Use [Google Play's refund process](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637) or the order controls in your Google Play account. Cancelling a subscription does not automatically refund a past charge. Except where Google Play or applicable law provides otherwise, partially used billing periods are not prorated or refunded.

## 2. Apple lifetime purchase

The Apple lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It has no recurring renewal to cancel.

To request a refund, use Apple's process at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) or contact Apple Support. Apple decides refund eligibility under its terms and applicable law. If Apple refunds or revokes the purchase, the paid entitlement may be removed.

If the purchase remains valid but does not appear, use **Restore Purchases** in the production App while signed into the Apple account that bought it.

## 3. Technical problems

Before requesting a refund for an entitlement problem:

- verify that you are using the store account that made the purchase;
- confirm the transaction is complete rather than pending;
- update the App and operating system where practical;
- use Restore Purchases; and
- restart the App.

If the problem continues, email lrodeveloperr@gmail.com with the platform, App version, a description of the problem and a redacted order or transaction reference. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, PINs, full invoices or confidential supplier records.

We can help diagnose entitlement delivery and may provide information to the Store, but we cannot guarantee or override a Store refund decision.

## 4. Mandatory rights

Nothing in this policy limits a refund, cancellation, warranty or other remedy that applicable law does not permit us or the Store to exclude. If this policy conflicts with mandatory law, the mandatory law controls.

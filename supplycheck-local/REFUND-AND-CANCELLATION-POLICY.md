# Refund and Cancellation Policy for SupplyCheck

**Effective date:** 12 August 2026  
**Last updated:** 12 August 2026

Apple and Google process SupplyCheck purchases through their Store systems. GoodUse Studios does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details. Store rules, this policy and mandatory consumer law govern cancellation and refund outcomes.

## 1. Android monthly subscription

### Cancel future renewal

The Android product is a monthly auto-renewing subscription. There is no annual SupplyCheck plan.

To cancel through Google Play:

1. Open [Google Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions) while signed in to the Google Account that made the purchase.
2. Select SupplyCheck.
3. Choose **Cancel subscription** and follow Google's instructions.

Google's current instructions are also available at [Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481).

Uninstalling or deleting the App does not cancel the subscription. Cancellation normally stops the next renewal and allows Pro access through the period already paid. A refund, revocation, account hold or other Store state can end access earlier. The renewal and paid-through dates shown by Google Play control.

### Request an Android refund

Use [Google Play's refund process and policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637) or the order controls in the purchasing Google Account. Cancelling does not automatically refund an earlier charge. Eligibility and any partial refund depend on Google Play's applicable process, GoodUse Studios' available Store controls, the circumstances, your location and mandatory law.

GoodUse Studios may help investigate an entitlement issue and, where the Store permits, support or process a request, but cannot promise a refund or override a mandatory Store outcome.

## 2. iOS one-time purchase

The iOS Pro product is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It is not a subscription, has no recurring charge and has no renewal to cancel.

To request an Apple refund, sign in at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) and choose **Request a refund**, or follow [Apple's refund instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223). Apple determines eligibility under its terms and applicable law. If Apple refunds or revokes the purchase, the App may remove the entitlement after Store verification.

If the purchase remains valid but does not appear, use **Restore purchases** in SupplyCheck while signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple's guidance for restoring a non-consumable purchase is available at [If you can't find or use your in-app purchases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108096).

## 3. Technical and entitlement problems

Before requesting a refund for an entitlement-delivery problem:

- confirm that the device uses the Store account that made the purchase;
- confirm the transaction is complete rather than pending;
- update the App and operating system where practical;
- use **Restore purchases**; and
- restart the App.

If the issue continues, email lrodeveloperr@gmail.com with the platform, App version, neutral error code and a redacted order or transaction reference. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, PINs, screenshots, supplier records, invoices, photos, databases or backup files.

GoodUse Studios can diagnose the App's entitlement handling using neutral information. We cannot guarantee a Store refund decision.

## 4. Free allowance and local records

The first five distinct completed deliveries are free once and do not reset monthly. The allowance creates no automatic charge. Deleting a completed record or all organisation content does not restore a free use.

Cancellation, refund or loss of Pro does not delete local records. Existing records, eligible corrections, reports, sharing, readable exports, backup/restore and recovery work remain available under the Terms of Use. A Store refund or revocation can remove the ability to authorize new paid-scope deliveries.

## 5. Mandatory rights

Nothing in this policy limits a refund, cancellation, warranty or other remedy that applicable law does not permit GoodUse Studios or the Store to exclude. Mandatory law controls where it conflicts with this policy.

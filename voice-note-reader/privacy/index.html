Voice Note Reader Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026. Voice Note Reader for Android is provided by WorksBien Studios Inc.

Voice notes and transcripts

Voice Note Reader reads an audio file when you open it or share it with the app. The app copies the audio into its private storage and transcribes it on your device. WorksBien receives neither your audio nor the resulting transcript through the app.

The app removes its temporary copy after transcription succeeds, or when you dismiss or cancel the note. If a note fails, the app keeps its temporary copy so you can retry. The app clears leftover temporary audio when it next starts. The transcript stays in memory for the current session. You can also clear the app's storage through Android settings.

If you choose Copy or Share, the transcript goes to the Android clipboard or the destination you select. The destination app's privacy practices apply after you share or paste the text there.

Free use and subscriptions

The app stores the month you used your free transcription on your device. If you subscribe, Google Play processes the payment and provides the app with purchase and subscription information to verify, restore, and manage your access. The app stores the time it last confirmed your subscription so you can keep using it during brief Play connection failures. WorksBien does not receive your payment card details. Google processes billing data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play.

Accounts and support

Voice Note Reader has no WorksBien account, advertising, or app analytics. If you contact us directly, we use the details you send to respond and keep them as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations.

For privacy questions or requests, email info@worksbienstudios.com.

# SWF One Support and Data Removal

**Updated:** 19 November 2026  
**Contact:** [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com)

## Playback support

SWF compatibility varies. If content does not open, confirm that the address or file is an SWF, try a local copy, and check whether the title requires unsupported ActionScript, network services or browser features. Do not email copyrighted SWF files unless you own them and are specifically asked to provide one.

## Remove local data

Open Android **Settings → Apps → SWF One → Storage & cache → Clear storage**. This removes SWF One's local settings, recent source, control layouts, cached content and compatible Flash local saves. Uninstalling the app also removes app-local data, subject to Android backup behavior.

## Remove cloud-sync data

Turn **Cloud sync** off in SWF One while the app is installed. SWF One then asks Android's backup transport to delete its cloud-backup entity. Android or the backup provider controls when that request is processed. Device-wide backup management is also available in Android or Google account settings.

SWF One has no developer account or WorksBien-operated cloud database, so there is no separate WorksBien account to delete.

## Subscription help

Use **Restore purchase** while signed into the purchasing Google Play account. Use **Manage subscription** in SWF One or Google Play's **Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions** page to cancel future renewal.

For support, email [lrodeveloperr@gmail.com](mailto:lrodeveloperr@gmail.com) with the app name, Android version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, private files or Google Play purchase tokens.
